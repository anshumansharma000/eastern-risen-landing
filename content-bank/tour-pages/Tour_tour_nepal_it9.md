# Tour To Nepal

Tour To Nepal
MUKTINATH DARSHA
BACK
Tour Duration: 03 Nights/ 04 Days
Destination:Kathmandu-Jomsom-Pokhara
DAY- 1 : Arrive Pokhara
Arrive Pokhara, you will be welcomed by our Representative who will assist you with the transfer and check-in to hotel, Evening at leisure. Overnight at Pokhara
DAY- 2 : Pokhara-(fly)Jomsom
After breakfast drive to airport to catch the flight to Jomsom and jeep drive to Muktinath Darshan and have holy bath, worship to Lord Narayan, visit Jwalamukhi and drive back to Jomsom. Overnight at Jomsom.
DAY- 3 : Jomsom-Pokhara
Early morning fly back to Pokhara and drive to Kathmandu via-Manakamana Devi temple by cable car. Overnight at Pokhara.
DAY- 4 : Pokhara Departure
In time transfer to airport for onwards journey. Tour Ends.
