# North East Tour (Sikkim Tour).

North East Tour (Sikkim Tour).
SIKKIM TOUR
BACK
Tour Duration: 06Nights/07Days
Destination: Gangtok-Lachung-Yumthang-Kalimpong
DAY- 01 : Arrival Bagdogra Airport/NJP Rly.station-Darjeeling (86kms/3hrs)
Arrival at NJP Railway station/ Bagdogra (IXB) airport. Meet & greet by our office representative, who will assist you to board on vehicle to Darjeeling. Check-in hotel / Later visit Tea Bouquet for Tea Testing & Chowrasta for shopping. O/N-Darjeeling.
DAY- 02 : Darjeeling-Gangtok (115kms/05-06hrs)
Early morning drive to Tiger Hill (8364ft/2550mts at around 4 AM), back to hotel, pass through Ghoom monastery. After breakfast check-out and enjoy Toy train ride and visit Turkvar Tea Garden & Factory. Drive to Gangtok. O/N-Gangtok.
DAY- 03 : Gangtok-Lachung (124kms/5.5 to 6.5hrs )
After breakfast drive to Lachung (8000ft/2439mts). En-route visit through Phodong monastery, Tashil view point, Kabi-Lungstok, Seven sister waterfall, Singhik view point. O/N-Lachung.
DAY -04 : Lachung-Yumthang-Gangtok (124kms/5.5 to 6.5hrs )
After breakfast proceed to Yumthang valley (11800ft/3598mts) (25kms/1.5hrs), en-route visit through Hot Springs, Sinbad Rhododendron Sanctuary. Later drive to Gangtok. O/N-Gangtok.
DAY -05 : Gangtok (124kms/5.5 to 6.5hrs )
After breakfast full day sightseeing of Gangtok like Tsomgo Lake, Cable car ride (subject to operation), Mamgyal Institute of Tibetology. Evening free for independent activities. O/N-Gangtok.
DAY -06 : Gangtok-Rumtek-Kalimpong.
After breakfast drive to Kalimpong, en-route visit through Rumtek Monastery, Flower garden (Pine view nursery). O/N-Kalimpong.
DAY -07 : Kalimpong-Bagdogra departure
After breakfast transfer to Airport/NJP railway station for onwards journey.
