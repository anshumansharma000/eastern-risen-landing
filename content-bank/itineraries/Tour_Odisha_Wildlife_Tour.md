# Odisha Wildlife Tour

Odisha Wildlife Tour
ODISHA WILDLIFE TOUR
BACK
Duration: 7 Nights/ 8 Days
Bhubaneswar-Bhitarkankika N.P-Similipal N.P-Chandipur
DAY-01 : Arrival Bhubaneswar
Arrival at Bhubaneswar. Check-in to the hotel. After noon orientation tour of Bhubaneswar. Over night at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-02 : Bhubaneswar
After breakfast full day sightseeing of Bhubaneswar including Lingaraj temple, Mukteswar temple, Rajarani temple, 64 yogini temple, Khandagiri & Udayagiri Caves, Tribal museum. Evening visit to the traditional market-Ekamra Haat. Overnight at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-03 : Bhubaneswar-Bhitarkanika N.P
After breakfast drive to Gupti. En-route visit through Ratnagiri-Lalitgiri & Udaygiri Buddhist circuit. Arrival at Gupti & check-in to the Resort. Evening nature walk in the river bank. Overnight at Gupti.
DAY-04 : Bhitarkanika N.P
After breakfast cruise in different creeks of Bhitarkanika to spot the crocodiles, different type of birds, Mangrove forest. Visit to Bagagahana-bird sanctuary, Sikarakathi-the hunting tower of ancient king, Shiv temple. Packed lunch will be served inside the boat. After lunch see crocodile breeding centre, museum, deer, water lizard etc. Overnight at Gupti.
DAY-05 : Bhitarkanika N.P-Similipal N.P
After breakfast enjoy the bullock cart ride/go for a village tour and drive to Similipal N.P. Overnight at Similipal N.P.
DAY-06 : Bhitarkanika N.P-Similipal N.P
After breakfast full day jungle trip of Similipal N.P (Bio reserve forest). See the beautiful water fall of Jaranda, Barehipani and spot the wildlife like peacock, deer, porcupine, elephant etc. Feel the sound of the forest. Overnight at Similipal N.P
DAY-07 : Similipal N.P-Chandipur
Morning go for a nature walk. After breakfast drive to Chandipur, en-route visit through Khirochora Gopinath temple, Balaramgadi-the fish market. After noon enjoy at the rare beach, where water recedes two times a day up to five kilometers. Overnight at Chandipur.
DAY-08 : Chandipur Departure
Early morning see the sun rise and have a morning walk in the rare beach. After breakfast drive to Balasore railway station/Bhubaneswar for onwards journey.
