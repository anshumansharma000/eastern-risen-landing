# South India Tour

South India Tour
EXOTIC KERALA
BACK
Duration: 8 Nights/ 9 Days
Destination: Cochin-Munner-Thekkady-Allepy
DAY-1 : Arrival COCHIN
Arrival at Cochi Airport/Railway station. You will be greeted by our representative who will assist you to board your vehicle to Hotel. Check-in to the Hotel. Halfday city tour includes, St. Francis Church, Dutch Palace, Jewish Synagogue, Jew Street, Chinese fishing nets, Fort Kochi beach etc. Overnight stay at Cochi.
DAY-2 : COCHIN-MUNNER (150 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Munnar (150 kms/4 hrs). En route enjoy the hair pin scenic crossing roads, beautiful valleys, tea gardens and waterfalls. Arrival at Munnar and check-in to the Hotel. After noon visit to Rajamala Wildlife Sanctuary. Overnight stay at Munnar.
DAY-3 : MUNNER
After breakfast whole day sightseeing of Matupetty dam, Echo point, pallivasal hydro electry poject, Devikulam, world highest tea plantation etc. Overnight stay at Munner.
DAY-4 : MUNNER-THEKKADY ( 130 kms)
After breakfast drive to Thekkady (Periyar W.L.S) (130 kms/3.5 hrs). En route enjoy the scenic beauty, tea gardens, dense woods, spice gardens etc. Arrival at Thekkady & check-in to the Hotel. Overnight stay at Thekkady.
DAY-5 : THEKKADY
After breakfast sightseeing of Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary. The beautiful artificial lake formed by the Mulla Periyar dam across the Periyar river adds additional charm to the Park. Have a charming boat riding inside the lake and if you are lucky, you can see the wildlife at closure view. Overnight stay at Thekkady.
DAY-7 : ALLEPY/KUMARAKOM
After breakfast cruise through small narrow canals and see the Paddy fields, Coconut lagoons, Coir villages & day to day life of the local people, small water lodged town-ship to enjoy the scenic beauty and to get glimpse of the unspoiled rural life of Kerala. Evening free for independent activities. Overnight stay at House boat.
DAY-07 : TRICHY – MADURAI (140 kms)
After breakfast, drive to Madurai (140 kms/2.5-3 hrs). Enroute visit to Cocoanut Rope work. Afternoon free for individual activities. Overnight stat at Madurai.
DAY-8 : ALLEPY/KUMARAKOM-COCHIN
After breakfast proceed to Cochin (64 kms/2 hrs). 2nd half free for independent activities.
DAY-9 : Departure COCHIN
In time transfer to Airport/Railway station for onwards journey.
