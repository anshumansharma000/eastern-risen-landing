# Bike Safari Tour

Bike Safari Tour
BIKE SAFARI TOUR-I
Duration: 14 Nights/15 Days
Destination: Delhi-Manali-Keylong-Sarchu-Leh-Pangong-Nubra Valley-Kargil-Sringar-Patnitop-Jammu
Day-1 : Arrival at DELHI –MANALI (585 kms)
Arrival at Delhi. Meet & greet by our office representative who will assist you to board your vehicle to Monali (585 kms/14 hrs). Arrival at Monali and check-in to the hotel. Overnight stay at Manali.
DAY- 2 : MANALI
Collect bikes and get checked for air and oil, Manali sightseeing on bike, vsit to Hidimba temple, Manali market, Fuel tank top-up. Overnight stay at Manali.
DAY- 3 : MANALI – KEYLONG (115 kms)
After early breakfast biking to Keylong.
Attractions -Rohtang La – At an altitude 13,051 ft ,its a high mountain pass on the eastern Pir Panjal Range of the Himalayas. It connects the Kullu Valley with the Lahaul and Spiti Valleys of Himachal Pradesh, India. Overnight stay at Keylong.
DAY- 4 : KEYLONG - SARCHU
After breakfast biking to Sarchu.
Attractions-Bara-lacha La – At 16,040 ft its a high mountain pass in Zanskar range, connecting Lahaul district in Himachal Pradesh to Ladakh in Jammu and Kashmir, situated along the Leh-Manali highway. Overnight stay at Sarchu.
DAY-5 : SARCHU – LEH (100 kms)
After breakfast biking to Leh.
Attractions-Gata Loops – Succession of 21 hairpin bends taking you from 13780 ft to 15302 ft-Nakee La - 16,230 ft -Lungalacha La - 16,601 ft-Morey Plains - Plains for about 40 kms straight at 14,763 ft. is part of Great Tibetan Chamathang plateau-Tanglang La – Second highest motorable mountain pass in India at 17,582 ft. Overnight stay at Leh.
DAY-6 : LEH MONASTRY CIRCUIT
After breakfast full day sightseeing of Shey Palace & Monastery – The Shey Monastery & Shey Palace was built in 1655. The main Shakyamuni Buddha statue in the monastery is a 12 metres (39 ft) icon covering three floors of the monastery. Thikseonastery – The Thiksey Monastery, situated at 12,000 ft, is a 12-storey complex, noted for its images and mural paintings of Buddhist art displayed variously in its stupas, statues, thangkas, wall paintings and swords.Hemis Monastery – The Hemis Monastery, situated on the south of Leh, which is one of the largest and most famous monasteries, belongs to the Drukpa order. Shanti Stupa - The Japanese for World Peace built the Shanti Stupa, at Changspa, on the hilltop, and was inaugurated by Dalai Lama in 1985. Leh Palace, Stok Gompa and Stok Palace . Overnight Leh.
DAY-7 : LEH – PANGONG TSO (154 kms)
After breakfast visit to Mighty Chang La -The Chang La at 17,800 ft is the third highest motorable road in the world-It is named after the sadhu Changla Baba, to whom the Pass temple is dedicated. Pangong Tso-It is the largest salt water lake in Asia situated at an altitude of about 14,300 ft-It is 134 km (83 mi) long and extends from India to China. 60% of the length of the lake lies in China. The lake is 5 km (3.1 mi) wide at its broadest point. During winter, the lake freezes completely, despite being saline water. It is a fantastic experience to see the lake at different times of the day. Moon rise and sun rise by the lake side are beautiful views. Overnight stay at Pngong Tso.
DAY-8 : PANGONG - LEH
After breakfast biking to Leh.
Man & Merak-Be the first one to travel along the banks of the Pangong Lake in Leh, right up to the villages of Man and Merak. Travel back to Leh & Visit Phyang Festival. Overnight stay Leh.
DAY-9 : LEH – NUBRA VALLEY
After breakfast biking to Nubra Valley-Khardung La (K-Top)-The pass on the Ladakh Range lies north of Leh and is the gateway to the Shyok and Nubra valleys.-Built in 1976, it was opened to motor vehicles in 1988 and has since seen many automobile, motorbike and mountain biking expeditions. Khardung La at 17,582ft is widely, but incorrectly, believed to be the world's highest motorable pass
Diskit Monastery-Diskit Monastery is situated on the hill, just above the flood plains of the Shayok River, on its right bank in the Diskit village (3,144 metres (10,310 ft)) in Nubra Valley.
It is the oldest (350 years) and largest Buddhist monastery (gompa) in the Nubra Valley, which was once part of the famous Caravan Route between Tibet . Overnight stay at Nubra Valley.
DAY-10 : NUBRA VALLEY - LEH
After breakfast visit to the sand dunes and take a ride on the double humped Bactrian camels. Ride back to Leh. Hunder-Hunder is about 7km further and it is the last point civilians can go. Further from Hunder is Siachen glaciers. Hunder is famous for the sand dunes and double humped Bactrian camels. These camels are believed to have been abandoned by the passing caravans centuries ago and have flourished there. Lachung temple-is the oldest temples in the Nubra Valley where a large idol of Tsong Khapa is deified and a Gelugpa yellow hat crowns the image. Overnight stay at Leh.
DAY-11 : LEH – KARGIL (234 kms)
After breakfast Biking to Kargil. Magnetic Hill- is a gravity hill located near Leh in Ladakh, India. The hill is alleged to have magnetic properties strong enough to pull cars uphill and force passing aircraft to increase their altitude in order to escape magnetic interference; in reality, the effect is an optical illusion created by the gravity hill. Pathar Sahib Gurudwara-The Gurdwara was built in 1517 to commemorate the visit of Guru Nanak Dev, the founder Guru of the Sikh faith, to the Ladakh region. Fotu La - At 13,605 ft, it is the highest point on the Leh - Srinagar highway. Lamayuru Monastery- Lamayuru is one of the largest and oldest gompas in Ladakh. Lamayuru is also called the Moonland View for the fantastic moonscapes it offers . Namika La – High pass at 12,204 ft. Overnight stay at Kargil.
DAY-12 : KARGIL – SRINAGAR (204 kms)
After breakfast sightseeing of Kargil and biking to Srinagar. Kargil – Visit Vijay Smarak to pay a tribute to soldiers who laid their lives for us in Kargil War in 1999. En route have a magnificent view of Kargil war Famous Peaks (Tiger Hill, Tololing). Drass - The second coldest inhabited place on Earth, with temperatures dropping to - 60 C in winter. The road after Kargil follows closely along the Line Of Control between India and Pakistan, and signs along the road remind travellers that they are "under enemy observation". Zoji La - Zoji La, at 11,578 ft from sea level, is the last mountain pass on the road from Ladakh to India’s infamous north-western part, Kashmir. After Zoji La the scenery changes from Ladakh's barren and brown to Kashmir's green forests. On the way visit Sonmarg – Sonamarg means ' meadow of gold ' has, as its backdrop, snowy mountains against a cerulean sky. the Sindh meanders along here and abounds with trout and mahseer, snow trout can be caught in the main river.
DAY-13 : SRINAGAR – PATANI TOP (157 kms)
After breakfast sight seeing of Srinagar – Visit to Dal Lake for boating, Visit to Shankaracharya Math. After sightseeing leave for Patani Top. Overnight stay at Patani Top.
DAY-14 : PATANI TOP – JAMMU (112 kms)
After breakfast sightseeing of Patani Top.
Patni Top, at an altitude of 6640ft, is a hill top tourist location in Udhampur district on the National Highway 1A, on the way from Udhampur to Srinagar. It is located in the Shiwalik belt of the Himalayas, and is in Jammu and Kashmir, India. The river Chenab flows in close proximity to this location.
After sightseeing biking to Jammu. Deposit the bike to send for native place. Overnight stay at Jammu.
DAY-15 : JAMMU- DEPARTURE…
After breakfast in time transfer to Railway Station / Airport for further destination
