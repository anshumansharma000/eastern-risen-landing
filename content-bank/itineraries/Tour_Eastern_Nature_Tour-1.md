# Eastern Nature Tour-I

Eastern Nature Tour-I
EASTERN NATURE TOUR-I
BACK
Duration: 6 Nights/ 7 Days
Bhubaneswar-Buddhist circuit (Lalitgiri-Ratnagiri-Udayagiri)-Bhitarkanika N.P
DAY-01 : Arrival Bhubaneswar
Arrival at Bhubaneswar. Check-in to the hotel. After noon orientation tour of Bhubaneswar. Over night at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-02 : BHUBANESWAR
After breakfast, full day sightseeing of Bhubaneswar including Lingaraj temple, Mukteswar temple, Rajarani temple, 64 yogini temple, Khandagiri & Udayagiri Caves, Tribal museum. Evening visit to the traditional market-Ekamra Haat. Overnight at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-03 : BHUBANESWAR-BUDHHIST CIRCUIT-BHITARKANIKA N.P
After breakfast drive to Bhitarkanika N.P. En-route visit through Ratnagiri-Lalitgiri & Udaygiri Buddhist circuit. Arrival at Gupti & check-in to the Resort. Evening nature walk in the river bank. Overnight at Gupti.
DAY-04 : BHITARKANIKA N.P
After breakfast cruise in different creeks of Bhitarkanika to spot the crocodiles, different type of birds, Mangrove forest. Visit to Bagagahana-bird sanctuary, Sikarakathi-the hunting tower of ancient king, Shiv temple. Packed lunch will be served inside the boat. After lunch see crocodile breeding centre, museum, deer, water lizard etc. Overnight at Gupti.
DAY-05 : GUPTI-EKAKULA-HABALIKATHI
Early morning tea/coffee, nature walk, . After breakfast board on boat to Ekakula (depends upon the tide time). Arrival at Ekakula, relax on the lonely beach. After lunch board on boat to Habalikathi. Arrival at Habalikathi. Evening relax on the lonely beach. Overnight at Habalikathi.
DAY-06 : HABALIKATHI-HUKITOLA-GUPTI
After early breakfast board on boat to Hukitola-the historical monument on the Bay of Bengal. Arrival at Hukitola, nature trek. Board on boat and come back to resort (depend on tide time). Overnight at Gupti.
DAY-07 : GUPTI Departure
In time transfer to airport/railway station for onwards journey.
