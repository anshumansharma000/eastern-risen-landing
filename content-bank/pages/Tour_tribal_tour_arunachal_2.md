# Tribal Tour(ARUNACHAL PRADESH)

Tribal Tour(ARUNACHAL PRADESH)
ARUNACHAL TRIBAL TOUR-II
BACK
Duration: 5 Nights/ 6 Days
Destination: Dibrugarh-Along-Daporijo-Ziro-Nameri-Tejpur-Guwahati
DAY-01 : Arrival - DIBRUGARH – ALONG
Arrival at Dibrugarh airport by Morning flight & transfer to Ferry Ghat & Cross the River Brahmaputra by country made ferry which takes 5 to 6 hrs in the Brahmaputra River to reach Along. The river Brahmaputra is the confluence of the Siang and Lohit river of Arunachal Pradesh. Along, the headquarter of west Saing district of Arunachal Pradesh, located at an altitude of 300m inhabitants of Adi(Gallong) tribes. Adi sub tribe which differ from each others like Adi( Gallong, minyong, pasi, padam, Bori, and Boker). Overnight stay in Along.
DAY-02 : ALONG - DAPORIJO
After breakfast drive to Daporijo (180 kms / 6 ½ hrs drive). Daporijo, the headquarter of the Upper Subansri district of Arunachal Pradesh located at an altitude of 600m is an inhabitant of Tagin tribes. En route visit to Hillmiri tribal villages. Overnight stay at Circuit House / tented / paying guest. (Very Very Basic)
DAY-03 : DAPORIJO - ZIRO (ARUNACHAL PRADESH)
Morning drive to Ziro (170 kms / 5 ½ hrs drive). En-route visit Hill Miri villages. Ziro is a small picturesque destination with Pine and Bamboo groves, villages, a craft centre and Tarin Fish Farm which is located at a high altitude and also to see are the indigenous method of rice cultivation. The languages spoken here are Apatani, Nishi, Hindi, Assamese and English. Overnight stay at Ziro.
DAY-04 : ZIRO
After breakfast full day sightseeing of Ziro, the headquarter of Lower Sabunsiri district of Arunachal Pradesh located at an altitude of 1560m, is a plateau surrounded by Apatani tribal villages, Dibbo- Hija- Bulla- Hari and Hong village. Hong village the second biggest and most organised tribal villages in Asia. The uniqueness of Apatani tribe is tatoo in women & man and its nose plug in women. Overnight stay at Ziro.
DAY-05 : ZIRO – NAMERI / TEZPUR
After breakfast drive to Nameri / Tezpur (280 kms / 8 hrs ). Overnight stay at Tezpur.
DAY-06 : NAMERI / TEZPUR –GUWAHATI AIRPORT - Departure
After breakfast drive to Guwahati airport (200 Kms / 4 hrs drive) to connect flight for onwards journey.
