# Central India Tour

Central India Tour
CENTRAL EXOTIC
BACK
Duration: 14 Nights/ 15 Days
Destination: Bhopal-Sanchi-Mandu-Indore-Ujjain-Shivpuri-Gwalior-Orchha-Bandhavgarh-Jabalpur-Bhedaghat-Kanha N.P-Pachmarhi-Pench-Nagpur
DAY- 1 : Arrival at BHOPAL
Arrival at Bhopal Railway Station/Airport, will be met by our office representative who will assist you to board your vehicle to Hotel. Check in to the Hotel & fresh up. Bhopal is a fascinating amalgam of scenic beauty, a proud heritage and modern urban planning. Sightseeing of beautiful mosques like Taj-ul-Masjid ( said to be one of the three largest mosques of world and the largest in Asia), Jama Masjid, quaint palaces like Shaukat Mahal and Sadar Manzil, Gauhar Mahal, State Museum, Birla Museum etc. O/N at Bhopal
DAY-2 : BHOPAL-SANCHI (48 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Sanchi (48 kms/1hrs) and sightseeing of Stupas, monasteries, temples & pillars dating from the 3rd century B.C to the 12th century A.D. Afternoon enjoy the boat ride in Bada Talab ( Upper Lake) of Bhopal. O/N at Bhopal
DAY- 3 : BHOPAL-BHOJPUR-BHIMBETKA (28-40 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Bhimbetka (68 kms/1.5 hrs). En route sightseeing of 11th century Shiva temple, Bhojeshwar at Bhojpur. After arrival at Bhimbetka sightseeing of rock-cut paintings that date back to the early stone age at the Vindhya mountain range which is covered by huge rocks. Back to Bhopal. If time permits sightseeing of Bhopal. O/N at Bhopal.
DAY- 4 : BHOPAL-MANDU (186 kms)
After early breakfast proceed to Mandu- the city of Joy ( 186 kms/3.5 hrs). Sightseeing of Darwazas, Jahaz Mahal, Hindola Mahal etc. O/N at Mandu.
DAY-5 : MANDU-INDORE-UJJAIN ( 99-55 kms)
After early breakfast proceed to Ujjain (154 kms/3 hrs). En route sightseeing of Indore like Rajwada, Lal bagh Palace, Kanch Mandir (Jain temple), Bada Ganapati (largest statue of Ganapati in the world)
Arrival at Ujjain and check in to the hotel. aleswara ( one of the 12 Jyotirlinga). O/N at Ujjain.
DAY-6 : UJJAIN
Whole day sightseeing of Ujjain like Mahakaleswar temple (one of the 12 Jyotirlinga), Bada Ganesh ji ka Mandir, Harsiddhi temple, Bhartrihari Caves, Kaliadeh Palace etc. O/N at Ujjain.
DAY-7 : UJJAIN-SHIVPURI (343 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Shivpuri. O/N at Shivpuri.
DAY-8 : SHIVPURI-GWALIOR (112 kms)
First half sightseeing of Shivpuri like Madhav Vilas Palace, Chhatris (Centaphs), Bhadaiya Kund- a scenic picnic spot. After Lunch proceed to Gwalior (112 kms/2 .5 hrs). O/N at Gwalior.
DAY-9 : GWALIOR-ORCHHA (118 kms)
First half sightseeing of Gwalior like Gwalior Fort, The Man Mandir Palace, Jai Vilas Palace and Museum etc. After lunch proceed to Orchha ( 118 kms/2.5 hrs. O/N at Orchha.
DAY-10 : ORCHHA-KHAJURAHO (170 kms)
First half sightseeing of Orchha like Fort Complex and Palaces, Rai Praveen Mahal, Jahangir Mahal etc. After lunch proceed to Khajuraho (170 kms/ 3.5 hrs). O/N at Khajuraho.
DAY-11 : KHAJURAHO
Whole day sightseeing of Khajuraho like temples of Khajuraho & its erotic sculptures, Laxman temple, Kandariya Mahadev temple, Jagadambi temple, The Chitragupta temple etc. O/N at Khajuraho.
DAY-12 : KHAJURAHO-BANDHAVGARH (237 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Bandhavgarh N.P( 237 kms/ 5 hrs). Park round in the evening. O/N at Bandhavgarh N.P.
DAY-13 : BANDHAVGARH
Whole day round the park and spot tigers, deer, sambar, nilgai etc. sightseeing of Fort, Bandhavgarh hill. Enjoy the elephant ride. O/N at Bandhavgarh.
DAY- 14 : BANDHAVGARH-JABALPUR (190 kms)
After early breakfast proceed to Jabalpur (190 kms/4.5 hrs). Check in to the hotel & fresh up. After lunch Sightseeing of Marble Rocks, Dhuandhar Falls by boating on the crystal-clear waters of the Narmada. O/N at Jabalpur
DAY-15 : JABALPUR Departure.
In time transfer to Airport/Railway station for onward
