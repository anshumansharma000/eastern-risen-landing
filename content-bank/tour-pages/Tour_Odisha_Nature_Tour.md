# Odisha Nature Tour

Odisha Nature Tour
ODISHA NATURE TOUR
BACK
Duration: 8 Nights/ 9 Days
Bhubaneswar-Buddhist circuit (Lalitgiri-Ratnagiri-Udayagiri)-Bhitarkanika N.P-Satkoshia-Bhubaneswar
DAY-01 : Arrival Bhubaneswar
Arrival at Bhubaneswar. Check-in to the hotel. After noon orientation tour of Bhubaneswar. Overnight at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-02 : BHUBANESWAR-BUDDHIST CIRCUIT-BHITARKANIKA N.P
After breakfast drive to Bhitarkanika N.P (Gupti). En-route visit through Ratnagiri-Lalitgiri & Udaygiri Buddhist circuit. Arrival at Gupti & check-in to the Resort. Evening nature walk in the river bank. Overnight at Gupti.
DAY-03 : BHITARKANIKA N.P
After breakfast cruise in different creeks of Bhitarkanika to spot the crocodiles, different type of birds, Mangrove forest. Visit to Bagagahana-bird sanctuary, Sikarakathi-the hunting tower of ancient king, Shiv temple. Packed lunch will be served inside the boat. After lunch see crocodile breeding centre, museum, deer, water lizard etc. Overnight at Gupti.
DAY-04 : GUPTI-EKAKULA-GUPTI
Early morning tea/coffee, nature walk, . After breakfast board on boat to Ekakula (depends upon the tide time). Arrival at Ekakula, relax on the lonely beach. After lunch board on boat to Gupti. Evening fee for independent activities. Overnight at Gupti.
DAY-05 : GUPTI-SATKOSIA WLS
Early morning nature walk on the river bank. After breakfast straight drive to Satkosia Wild life Sanctuary. Check-in to the forest guest house. Overnight at Satkosia WLS.
DAY-06 : SATKOSIA WLS
Morning go for a nature walk inside the forest. After breakfast enjoy the boat cruising in Satkosia Gorge and spot crocodiles. Overnight at Satkosia WLS.
DAY-07 : SATKOSIA-SAPTASAJYA-BHUBANESWAR
Morning you can enjoy the elephant ride. After breakfast drive to Bhubaneswar, en-route visit through Saptasajya-one of the best scenic destination. Evening free for independent activities. Overnight at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-08 : BHUBANESWAR
After breakfast full day sightseeing of Bhubaneswar including Lingaraj temple, Mukteswar temple, Rajarani temple, 64 yogini temple, Khandagiri & Udayagiri Caves, Tribal museum. Evening visit to the traditional market-Ekamra Haat. Overnight at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-09 : BHUBANESWAR Departure
In time transfer to Airport/Railway station for onwards journey.
