# Definitive Google Stitch prompt

Paste the prompt below into a new Stitch project. Add `PRODUCT.md` to the canvas as project context if the interface allows file or text context.

---

Design a high-fidelity, responsive website and clickable prototype for **Eastern Risen**, a specialist Indian travel and expedition company. Treat the attached `PRODUCT.md` as the product source of truth.

## Business objective

The website must establish trust, showcase deep destination knowledge, make a large library of detailed tours easy to explore, and convert qualified visitors into thoughtful trip-planning enquiries. This is an enquiry-led travel business, not an instant-booking marketplace and not a discount OTA.

Eastern Risen is rooted in Odisha and eastern India but operates journeys across India, Nepal, Bhutan, Sri Lanka, Tibet and parts of Southeast Asia. Its strongest themes are Odisha, wildlife, tribal and cultural travel, Buddhist and spiritual journeys, textiles and crafts, festivals, trekking, bike journeys and tailor-made travel.

The brand promise is: **“We know these places deeply, and we will take care of you.”**

## Creative direction

Create a visual direction called **Quiet Expedition Luxury with an Odisha soul**.

The experience should feel grounded, exploratory, human, culturally respectful, calm, experienced, trustworthy and quietly premium. Combine the editorial confidence of a boutique expedition journal with subtle inspiration from Odisha’s landscapes, temple geometry, textiles and craft traditions.

Do not make it look like a generic travel agency, airline portal, hotel-booking marketplace or luxury resort template. Avoid bright blue travel gradients, discount banners, fake urgency, aeroplane-path clichés, crowded card grids, oversized rounded pills, autoplay carousels, glassmorphism and decorative “ethnic” stereotypes.

## Visual system

Preserve and elevate the original site's orange-led colour identity. Use this palette:

- Heritage orange `#E3701D` for original brand recognition, logo details, rules, highlights and selected states
- CTA rust `#B95016` for accessible primary buttons and high-emphasis actions
- CTA rust hover `#963E12` for button hover and pressed states
- Sunlit apricot `#F1A15D` for gentle highlights and warm editorial graphics
- Warm ivory `#FFF9F2` as the primary page background
- Sand cream `#F3E7D5` for alternating editorial sections
- White `#FFFFFF` for cards, overlays and clear surfaces
- Ink charcoal `#25221F` for primary text, navigation and footer contrast
- Warm grey `#6A625C` for secondary text
- Sand border `#DED2C3` for dividers, inputs and quiet component boundaries
- Expedition slate `#244E63`, refined from the original blue family, as the single cool counterpoint for links, maps and informational accents

Use ivory, sand and white across roughly 70% of the interface, charcoal/grey for approximately 20%, orange/rust for 8-10%, and slate for no more than 2-3%. Do not reproduce the old site's large saturated orange outer background or make every component orange. Do not place small white text on Heritage Orange; use Ink Charcoal on the bright orange or use CTA Rust with white text. Exclude legacy magenta from the core UI palette. The result should remain recognisably Eastern Risen while feeling calm, cohesive, editorial and premium.

Use an editorial serif similar to **Newsreader or Fraunces** for display headings and a clean sans-serif similar to **Inter or Manrope** for body text, navigation and UI. Use large expressive headings, generous whitespace, readable body text, thin dividers and restrained borders.

Photography should feel documentary and atmospheric: people naturally situated in landscapes, authentic local guides and interactions, Odisha’s coast, forests, crafts, temples and Buddhist heritage, wildlife in natural habitat, Himalayan journeys, textiles, hands, food, boats, roads and rituals. Mix cinematic landscapes with intimate details. Do not use oversaturated generic stock collages.

Use subtle paper grain and very light abstracted Odisha architectural or textile geometry. Keep ornament restrained. Motion should be gentle and purposeful.

## Build a coherent design system and these linked screens

Create polished **desktop and mobile layouts** for:

1. Homepage
2. Tour collection/search page
3. Tour detail page
4. Odisha destination page
5. About and sustainability page
6. Plan a Journey enquiry page

Use consistent navigation, footer, type scale, spacing, buttons, cards, form controls, filter controls, accordions, badges and image treatments across every screen. Connect the main interactions into a clickable prototype.

## Global navigation

Primary navigation:

- Tours
- Destinations
- Travel Styles
- About
- Sustainability
- Journal
- **Plan a Journey** as the primary CTA

Include search and a compact contact option. Design a clear mobile menu that exposes destination and travel-style categories without becoming crowded.

## Homepage

Create a cinematic but fast-loading hero. Use this copy:

**Journeys that bring you closer.**

“Private and small-group journeys across India and its neighbouring lands, designed with local knowledge and personal care.”

Primary CTA: **Explore journeys**  
Secondary CTA: **Plan a private tour**

Follow the hero with:

- A curated Signature Journeys section showing no more than six tours
- A concise Why Eastern Risen section with local expertise, tailor-made itineraries, responsible travel, specialist access and personal support
- An editorial Explore by Travel Style section for Wildlife and Nature, Tribal and Cultural, Adventure and Trekking, Buddhist and Spiritual, Textile and Craft, and Festivals
- A distinctive feature section titled **“Our home ground: Odisha”**, with strong regional storytelling and a layered editorial layout
- A human founder/local-expertise story with an authentic portrait
- A sustainability and community section grounded in specific practices
- A restrained verified-testimonials and affiliations section
- A Field Notes / Journal preview
- A final planning CTA: **“Where would you like to go next?”**

Use realistic sample tours from the legacy catalog:

- Odisha Golden Triangle — 3 nights / 4 days
- Eastern Nature Tour — Odisha Buddhist circuit and Bhitarkanika
- Sikkim Delight — 10 nights / 11 days
- Eastern Textile Tour — 10 nights / 11 days
- North East Wildlife Tour — 8 nights / 9 days
- Bhutan Tour IV — 8 nights / 9 days

## Tour collection page

Create a refined editorial collection rather than an ecommerce grid. Include:

- Introductory copy and collection title
- Filters for destination, travel style, duration, ideal season and activity level
- Clear selected-filter state and reset action
- Search within tours
- Tour cards containing image, destination/region, tour name, duration, one-sentence promise and View Journey action
- A useful mobile filter sheet
- A no-results state with suggestions

Use a mix of card proportions and editorial pacing while keeping scanning predictable.

## Tour detail page

Use **Odisha Golden Triangle** as the representative tour.

Include:

- Immersive hero with tour name and concise promise
- Duration, region, travel style, starting/ending points, ideal season and activity level
- Route summary: Bhubaneswar → Konark → Chilika (Satapada) → Puri
- Tour highlights
- Four-day itinerary displayed as an elegant timeline on desktop and accessible accordion on mobile
- Route/map treatment
- Accommodation, transport, meals, inclusions and exclusions
- Responsible-travel notes
- Gallery
- Related journeys
- Sticky but unobtrusive **Plan this journey** CTA

The page must remain comfortable to read when an itinerary contains 15–20 days.

## Odisha destination page

Make this the most distinctive editorial screen. Include:

- Sense-of-place hero
- A narrative introduction to Odisha
- Temples and heritage, Buddhist sites, coast and Chilika, wildlife and forests, textiles and crafts, and community/cultural journeys
- Best time to visit
- Signature Odisha tours
- Practical guidance
- Related field notes
- Plan an Odisha Journey CTA

Use layered photography, quiet typographic drama and subtle regional geometry without becoming ornamental.

## About and sustainability page

Combine human storytelling and evidence-based trust:

- Eastern Risen company story
- Founder story for Bijan Kumar Muduli, clearly marked as content requiring final verification
- Planning philosophy and personal service
- Current team/guide placeholders
- Verified-credentials component with a “verification required” content state
- Sustainability practices, cultural respect, wildlife standards and community benefit
- ATOAI sustainability-pledge document treatment
- Contact/offices CTA

Do not invent awards, reviews, statistics, memberships or sustainability claims.

## Plan a Journey page

Create a warm, conversational enquiry experience—not a cold corporate form.

Include:

- Name
- Email
- Optional phone/WhatsApp
- Country
- Destinations of interest
- Travel styles/interests
- Approximate dates or “I’m flexible”
- Number and type of travellers
- Preferred trip length
- Existing tour of interest
- Optional accessibility, dietary or mobility needs
- Message
- Privacy consent
- Clear response-time expectation

Group fields into approachable steps or sections. Show validation, focus, error and success states. Do not request unnecessary sensitive information.

## Accessibility and responsive behavior

Design toward WCAG 2.2 AA. Use strong contrast, semantic hierarchy, visible keyboard focus, meaningful labels, 44px minimum touch targets, non-colour status indicators and reduced-motion-friendly interactions. Nothing important may rely on hover. Sticky mobile actions must not obscure content.

The mobile design must feel intentionally composed, not like a collapsed desktop layout. Keep long tour content legible and the primary planning action easy to reach.

## Output expectations

Produce a coherent high-fidelity web design system and connected prototype, not isolated mood-board screens. Show reusable components and consistent states. Use realistic travel copy and data, but clearly mark business facts, testimonials, credentials, prices and dates that require verification. Do not invent pricing.

The final result should feel like a premium specialist expedition journal with deep Indian roots, clear modern usability and unmistakable Eastern Risen character.
