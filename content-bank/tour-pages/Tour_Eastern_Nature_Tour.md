# Eastern Nature Tour

Eastern Nature Tour
EASTERN NATURE TOUR
BACK
Duration: 3 Nights/ 4 Days
Bhubaneswar-Buddhist circuit (Lalitgiri-Ratnagiri-Udayagiri)-Bhitarkanika N.P
DAY-01 : Arrival Bhubaneswar
Arrival at Bhubaneswar. Check-in to the hotel. After noon city tour including temples of Lingaraj, Mukteswar, Rajarani etc. Evening visit to the traditional market ‘Ekamra Haat’. Over night at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-02 : BHUBANESWAR-BHITARKANIKA N.P
Early morning drive to Bhitarkanika N.P. Check-in to the Resort and have your breakfast. After breakfast cruise in different creeks of Bhitarkanika to spot crocodiles, different type of birds, Mangrove forest etc. Visit to Bagagahana-bird sanctuary, Sikarakathi-the hunting tower of ancient king, Shiv temple. Packed lunch will be served inside the boat. After lunch see crocodile breeding centre, museum, deer, water lizard etc. Overnight at Gupti.
DAY-03 : BHITARKANIKA NP-BHUBANESWAR
After breakfast drive to Bhubaneswar. 2nd half sightseeing of Nandankanan zoological park, Khandagiri & Udayagiri caves. Evening free for independent activities. Overnight at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-04 : BHUBANESWAR Departure
In time transfer to Airoport/Railway station for onwards journey.
