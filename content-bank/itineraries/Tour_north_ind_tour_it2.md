# North India Tour

North India Tour
HOLY VAISHNODEVI
BACK
Duration: 4 Night/5 Days
Destination: Jammu-Katra-Vaishnodevi-Patnitop
DAY-1 : Arrival JAMMU-KATRA (58 kms)
Arrival at Jammu Airport/Railway station. You will be greeted by our representative who will assist you to board the vehicle. Drive to Katra(58kms/2 hrs), check-in to the Hotel. Spend the evening at leisure. O/N stay at Katra.
DAY-2 : KATRA-VAISHNODEVI (14 kms)
After breakfast in-time transfer to Helipad for Mata Vaishnodevi darshan. After darshan return to your hotel late at night. O/N stay at Katra
DAY-3 : KATRA-PATNITOP (112 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Patnitop (112 kms/2024 mtrs/4 hrs.Arrival at Patnitop and check-in to the hotel. 2nd half enjoy its lush green meadow and pine tree which resembles with Gulmarg. It offers beautiful picnic spots, peaceful walks and breathtaking views of the mountains cape of the Chenab basin. Enjoy skiing, sledging etc. O/N stay at Katra.
DAY-4 : KATRA-JAMMU (58 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Jammu (58 kms/2hrs). After lunch sightseeing of temples like Ranbireswar, Raghunath temple, Pear khol etc., Amar Mahal palace Museum etc. Evening free for individual activities. O/N stay at Jammu.
DAY-5 : JAMMU Departure
In-time transfer to Airport/Railway station for onwards journey.
