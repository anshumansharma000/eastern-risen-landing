# Central India Tour

Central India Tour
CENTRAL NATURE’S TREASURE
BACK
Duration: 10 Nights/ 11 Days
Destination: Jabalpur-Bandhavgarh-Kanha-Pachmarhi-Pench-Nagpur
DAY- 1 : Arrival at JABALPUR
Arrival at Jabalpur Railway Station/Airport, will be met by our office representative who will assist you to board your vehicle to Hotel. Check in to the Hotel & fresh up. Sightseeing of Marble Rocks, Dhuandhar Falls by boating on the crystal-clear waters of the Narmada, Evening free for independent activities. O/N at Jabalpur
DAY-2 : JABALPUR-BANDHAVGARH (190 kms)
Morning sightseeing of Madan Mahal Fort, Rani Durgavati Memorial and Museum, Chausath Yogini Temple. After early lunch proceed to Bandhavgarh (190 kms/4 hrs). Check in to the N.P . O/N at Bandhavgarh.
DAY- 3 : BANDHAVGARH
Whole day round the park and spot tigers, deer, sambar, nilgai etc. sightseeing of Fort, Bandhavgarh hill. Enjoy the elephant ride. O/N at Bandhavgarh.
DAY- 4 : BANDHAVGARH-KANHA ( 240 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Kanha N.P (240 kms/5hrs). Check in to the N.P. O/N at Kanha N.P
DAY-5 : KANHA N.P
Whole day round the Park by jeep and spot the wild life like tigers, blackbuck, chital, Indian Bison etc. Bamni Dadar-known as the sunset point. O/N at Kanha N.P.
DAY-6 : KANHA N.P – PACHMARHI (337 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Pachmarhi –the only hill station in Madhya Pradesh(337 kms/6.5 hrs). Evening scenic beauty of Pachmarhi. O/N at Pachmarhi.
DAY-7 : PACHMARHI
Whole day sightseeing of Bee Falls- one of the most enchanting water falls of Pachmarhi, Priyadarshini Point ( a vantage viewing point), Cave Shelters, Pandava Gufa etc. O/N at Pachmarhi.
DAY-8 : PACHMARHI - PENCH (290 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Pench N.P (290 kms/6 hrs). Check in to the Pench N.P. O/N at Pench N.P.
DAY-9 : PENCH
Whole day enjoy the Jungle Safari-the major attraction of the Park, Elephant Rides, Boating at the Pench reservoir. O/N at Pench N.P.
DAY-10 : PENCH-NAGPUR (110 kms)
Early morning nature walk inside the park. Forenoon proceed to Nagpur (110 kms/ 2.5 hrs). Evening free for independent activities. O/N stay at Nagpur.
DAY-11 : NAGPUR Departure
In time transfer to Airport/Railway station for onwards journey.
