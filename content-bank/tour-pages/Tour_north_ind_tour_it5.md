# North India Tour

North India Tour
LEH-LADAKH
BACK
Duration: 05Nights/06Days
Destination: Leh-Khardongla Pass-Alchi-Uletokpo-Lamayuru
DAY-1 : Arrival Leh.
Arrival at Leh airport. Meet & greet by our office representative, who will assist you to board on vehicle to Hotel. Full day for the acclimatization which is 3500mtrs above sea level. Evening short walk around Leh market. O/N-Leh.
DAY-2 : Leh-Khardongla Pass-Leh (45kms/02-03hrs one way)
After breakfast full day excursion to Khardongla pass which is the highest motorable pass in the world (5600mtrs) and has been recorded in the Gunnies Book of world records. At the top enjoy the nature beauty of snow capped mountain. Evening back to the Hotel. O/N-Leh.
DAY-3 : Leh-Alchi-Uletokpo (75kms/4.5hrs one way)
After breakfast leave for Uletokpo camping which is one of the most beautiful camping in the Ladakh region, en-route visit Spituk Monastery which is just 5 kms from Leh town. Continue drive to Alchi Monastery-the only Monastery in the Ladakh on flat ground. Evening enjoy to sit on the bank of river coming down from the Ridzong side. O/N-At Camp.
DAY-4 : Uletokpo-Lamayuru-Leh (180kms/5.5 hrs )
After breakfast leave Uletokpo and drive to Lamayuru via Khaltsi. From here another 22kms drive will take to Lamayuru. The oldest and spectacularly set Lamayuru monastery founded in the 10th Century. Visit the monastery. In the late afternoon drive back to Leh which will take about 4/5hours drive, on the way back we stop at magnetic hill. O/N-Leh.
DAY-5 : Leh-Pangong Lake-Leh (290kms/ )
Leave early morning at 5AM to Pangong Lake, en-route Changla pass (5486mtrs). Enjoy the beautiful panoramic view of the villages below the pass. After crossing the pass halt for picnic lunch at Tsoltak-the summer pasture land of the yaks. Arrive Durubuk and from there a short drive takes you to the western shore of the lake. Enjoy the landscape in the back drop of the Lake. This famous blue brackish lake of Pangong is 5/6 kms wide and over 144kms long with half of it’s running the other side of the “INDO CHINA BORDER”. One rarely feels so close to nature and environment and the scenery is unforgettable. In the late afternoon drive back to Leh. O/N-Leh.
DAY-6 : Leh Departure
After breakfast in-time transfer to Airport/NJP railway station for onwards journey.
