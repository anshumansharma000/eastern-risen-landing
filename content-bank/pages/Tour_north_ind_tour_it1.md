# North India Tour

North India Tour
HEAVENLY HIMACHAL
BACK
Duration: 6 Night/7 Days
Destination: Chandigarh-Shimla-Kullu-Manali
DAY-1 : Arrival CHANDIGARH
Arrival at Chandigarh Airport/Railway station. You will be greeted by our representative who will assist you to board the vehicle. Drive to Shimla(125kms/3 hrs), check-in to the Hotel. Spend the evening at leisure. Overnight stay at Shimla.
DAY-2 : SHIMLA-KUFRI(16 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Kufri. Visit through Indira Bungalow & Himalayan Zoo. After lunch sightseeing of Jakhu hill, Mall Road, Church and Scandal Point. Evening free for individual activities. Overnight stay at Shimla.
DAY-3 : SHIMLA-MANALI (280 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Manali (280 kms/1926 mtrs/9-10hrs), Enroute drive past Beas-Sutlej Link Project & Pandoh Dam. Lunch at wayside restaurant. See the Shawl factory at Kullu. Arrive at Manali and check-in to the Hotel. Overnight stay at Manali
DAY-4 : MANALI
After breakfast, go for a half day sightseeing tour and visit Vashistha temple &Kund, Hadimba temple, Manu temple, Gayatri temple. 2nd half free for individual activities. Overnight stay at Manali.
DAY-5 : MANALI-RATANG PASS (52 kms)
After breakfast, go for an excursion to snow point on Rohtang Pass (4112 mts/52 kms/2 hrs). Visit through Kothi and Rahalla falls. Return to Manali. Overnight stay at Manali.
DAY-6 : MANALI-CHANDIGARH ( 397 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Chandigarh (397 kms/11-12 hrs). Lunch at way-side Dhaba. Arrive at Chandigarh and check-in to the Hotel. Overnight stay at Chandigarh.
DAY-7 : CHANDIPARGH Departure
In-time transfer to Airport/Railway station for onwards journey.
