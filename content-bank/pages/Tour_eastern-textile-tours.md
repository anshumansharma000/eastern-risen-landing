# Eastern Textile Tour

Eastern Textile Tour
EASTERN TEXTILE TOUR
Duration: 10 Nights/ 11 Days
Destination: Bhubaneswar-Nuapatana-Dhenkanal-Sonepur-Sambalpur-Bargad-Sonepur-Bolangir-Baliguda-Berhampur-Gopalpur-Chilika Lake
DAY-1 : Arrival BHUBANESWAR
Arrival at Bhubaneswar Airport/Railway station. Transfer to Hotel. Half day sightseeing of Bhubaneswar including Lingaraja, Parsurameswar, Mukeswar, Rajarani Temple.Overnight stay at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-2 : BHUBANESWAR-MANIABAND-NUAPATNA –DHENKANAL.
After breakfast, drive to Nuapatana & Mania Bandah (110 kms/ 3 hrs), the largest weaver village (Silk/Pata Saree) & the only Buddhist village in Odisha. Experience the Handloom work of Pata/Silk sarees. After lunch drive to Sadeibareni for Dokra work, the typical tribal handicrafts of Odisha.
Overnight stay at Dhenkanal.
DAY-3 : DHENKANAL-SAMBALPUR
After breakfast drive to Sambalpur, en route visit through rock painting at Redhakhol. Evening visit to Samaleswari temple. Overnight stay at Sambalpur
DAY-4 : SAMBALPUR-BARPALI
After breakfast drive to Barpali weekly market( Commence only on Friday),visit to different weaver village like Barpali-the largest weaver village in western Odisha. Evening visit to different handloom shop. Overnight stay at Sambalpur.
DAY-5 : SAMBALPUR-SONEPUR-BALANGIR
After breakfast drive to Sonepur, en route visit through Huma-the leaning temple. See the most popular Sambalpuri silk weaver villages at Sonepur.Drive to Balangir and overnight.
DAY-6 : BALANGIR-BALIGUDA
After breakfast proceed to Baliguda, en route covering some weaver village. Visit some Kutia Kondh tribal village. Overnight stay at Baliguda.
DAY-7 : BALIGUDA-TAPTAPANI-GOPALPUR
After breakfast visit Taptapani-the hot sulphuric hot spring water, Padmanavpur popular for matha handloom work. Then drive to Gopalpur on sea. Overnight stay at Gopalpur on sea.
DAY-8 : GOPALPUR-BERHAMPUR-BARKUL
Early morning sunrise at Gopalpur-on-sea. After breakfast proceed to Berhampur and see the handloom work & showroom at Silk City Berhampur. Then proceed to Barkul. Afternoon enjoy the boat ride in chilika lake. Overnight stay at Barkul.
DAY-9 : BARKUL-BHUBANESWAR
Early Morning boating in side chilka lake, explore the Nalabana Island, Kalijai Island. After lunch drive to Bhubaneswar en route visit through Onlasuni & Routpada weaver village. Overnight stay at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-10 : BHUBANESWAR
After breakfast full day sightseeing of Bhubaneswar including Khandagiri & Udayagiri Caves,Tribal Museum, State Museum ( Monday Closed),64 Yogini Temple. Evening free for independent activities.
DAY-11 : BHUBANESWAR Departure
In time transfer to Airport for onwards journey.
