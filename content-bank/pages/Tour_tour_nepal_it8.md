# Tour To Nepal

Tour To Nepal
KATHMANDU NATURE TOUR WITH LUMBINI
BACK
Tour Duration: 07 Nights/ 08 Days
Destination:Kathmandu-Chitwan N.P-Lumbini-Pokhara
DAY- 1 : Arrive Kathmadu
Arrive Kathmandu, you will be welcomed by our Representative who will assist you with the transfer and check-in to hotel, rest of the day leisure for shopping. Overnight at Kathmandu.
DAY- 2 : Kathmandu
After breakfast full day sightseeing of Kathmandu including Pashupatinath, Baudhanath, Swayambhunath, Budhanikantha (sleeping Bishnu) Durbar Square (optional tour Casino). Overnight at Kathmandu.
DAY- 3 : Kathmandu-Chitwan N.P
After breakfast drive to Chitwan National Park (165 kms). On the way visit Manokamana temple (Only possible by Car). For Bus directly to Chitwan. Oveernight at forest Resort.
DAY- 4 : Chitwan N.P (Jungle Activities)
After breakfast, full day jungle activities (National Park). Overnight at forest Resort.
DAY- 5 : Chitwan N.P-Lumbini
After breakfast drive to Lumbini. Arrival at Lumbini and free for leisure. Overnight at Lumbini.
DAY- 6 : Lumbini
After breakfast, full day sightseeing of Lumbini and transfer to Pokhara. Overnight at Pokhara.
DAY- 7 : Pokhara
After breakfast, full day sightseeing tour of Pokhara (Fewa lake, Devis falls, Seti river National Museum, Mahendra Cave, Gupteswhwor Mahadev Cave and many more on the way etc. Overnight at Pokhara.
DAY- 8 : Pokhara-Kathmandu (210 kms/4.5 hrs) Departure
After early breakfast drive to Kathmandu (210 kms/4.5 hrs) and in-time transfer to airport for onwards journey. Tour Ends.
