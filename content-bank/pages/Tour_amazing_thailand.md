Amazing Thiland

Duration: 4 Nights/ 5 Days
Bangkok-day Safari World or dream world with snow town-Pattaya-Coral island
DAY-01 :Arrival in Bangkok
Arrival at Bangkok, Our Representative will assist you the transfer and check –in to hotel. Overnight at hotel.
DAY-02 : Full day Safari World or dream world with snow town
After breakfast take a day-long adventure trip to either safari world with marine park or Dream world. see live animal performances at safari world and explore the largest collection of wild and aquatic life at marine park or let your fantasies became reality dream world with magical 4D show and fun rides for all ages . Lunch is include at all parks. Overnight at hotel. .
DAY-03 :Sightseeing in Bangkok and Transfer to Pattaya
After breakfast at the hotel, proceed for a half day city & temple tour ending with a visit to the Gens factory. In the afternoon transfer and check – into hotel in Pattaya. Evening at leisure.
DAY-04 : Coral island with Lunch
After breakfast proceed for coral island tour, famous for colorful coral reef and marine life. Ride into a speed boat to Coral island. Hop in a glass bottom boat to view marine life. Lunch will also be provided. Enjoy the optional water sports like parasailing, snorkeling and windsurfing. Overnight at hotel.
DAY-05 : Transfer & Departs from Bangkok
After breakfast at hotel, enjoy the day at leisure for last minute shopping and other activities of your choice .check out from hotel and transfer to the airport.
