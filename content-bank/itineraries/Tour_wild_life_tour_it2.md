# Wildlife Tour

Wildlife Tour
“CENTRAL WILD LIFE TOUR-I”
BACK
Duration: 13 Nights/ 14 Days
Destination: Delhi-Nagpur-Tadob N.P-Kanha N.P-Bandhabgarh N.P-Khajuraho-Jhansi-Agra-Bharatpur
DAY- 01 : Arrival DELHI
Arrival at Delhi Airport/Railway station. You will be met by our office representative who will assist you to board your vehicle to hotel. Half day sight seeing of Delhi including Qutubminar, Humayun Tomb & India gate. Overnight stay at Delhi.
DAY- 02 : DELHI - NAGPUR –TADOBA N.P
In-time transfer to airport for connect flight to Nagpur. Arrival at Nagpur and transfer to Tadoba National Park. Evening free for independent activities. Overnight stay at Tadoba National Park.
Day -03 : TADOBA N.P
Early morning tea/coffee, transfer to forest entry gate for safari inside the National Park. Afternoon jeep safari inside the National park or visit to the villages. Overnight stay at Tadoba National Park.
DAY-04 : TADOBA N.P - NAGPUR
After breakfast drive back to Nagpur. Afternoon halfday city tour. Overnight stay at Nagpur.
DAY-05 : NAGPUR – KANHA N.P
After breakfast drive to Kanha National Park. Arrival at Kanha N.P and check-in to the hotel. Evening enjoy the nature view. Overnight stay at Kanha National Park.
DAY-06 : KANHA N.P
After early breakfast, Jeep safari inside the National Park. Afternoon jeep safari inside National Park. Overnight stay at kanha National Park.
DAY-07 : KANHA N.P – BANDHABGARH N.P
After breakfast drive to Bandhabgarh National Park. Evening free for independent activities.Overnight stay Bandhabgarh National Park.
DAY-08 : BANDHABGAR N.P
After early breakfast, Jeep safari inside the National Park. Afternoon jeep safari inside National Park. Overnight stay at Bandhabgarh National Park.
DAY-09 : BANDHABGAR-KHAJURAHO
After breakfast drive to Khajuraho. Afternoon free for indivisual activities. Overnight stay at Khajuraho.
DAY-10 : KHAJURAHO
After breakfast ,sightseeing of western & eastern group of temples. Overnight stay at Khajuraho.
DAY- 11 :KHAJURAHO-JHANSI-AGRA
After breakfast ,In time transfer to Jhansi, to board on train to Agra. Overnight stay at Agra.
DAY-12 : AGRA- BHARATPUR
After breakfast sightseeing of Taj Mahal, Agra Fort & Itimad –ud-daulah. Drive to Bharatpur Koelado Ghana National Park. En-route visit to Fatehpur Sikri. Overnight stay at Bharatpur.
DAY-13 : BHARATPUR – DELHI
Early morning visit to Koelado National Park. After lunch drive to Delhi. Overnight stay at Delhi.
DAY-14 : DELHI– Departure
In time Transfer to the airport/railway station for connect flight to further destination.
