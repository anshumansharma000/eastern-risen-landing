# Tour To Tibet

Tour To Tibet
TIBET TOUR-I
Duration: 7 Nights/8 Days
Destination: Kathmadu-Nayalam-Lhatse-Xigatse-Gyantse-Lhasa
DAY-01 : KATHMANDU(1300M) – NYALAM (3750M)( 156KM, APPROX 08 HRS DRIVE:)
Early morning scenic drive through the typically beautiful Nepalese country side to Kodari (Nepal – Tibet border), on arrival at Kodari clients has to go through the immigration formalities in Nepal Immigration and check into the Chinese side for Chinese immigration formalities and drive to Zhangmu and further to Nyalam. Over night stay at Guest House in twin sharing / dormitory.
DAY-02 : NYALAM – LHATSE (4350M),(244KM/ APPROX DRIVE 07-08 HRS)-
Day drive to the highest plateau of the world with typical landscape offering the sheer feeling of being on the roof of the world crossing over the 5,050 meters LALUNGA pass, from where you can view the breathtaking mountain range from Mt.Cho Oyu (8,201m) to Mt.Xishapagma (8,012m) dwarfed by the vastness of Tibetan plateau (if weather permits). One can also take a magnificent view of Mt. Everest (8,848m) from Tingri/ Lhatse. Overnight stay in Guest house in twin sharing / dormitory.
DAY-03 : LHATSE - XIGATSE (3,900M)(244KM, APPROX 06-08 HRS DRIVE: )
Scenic drive through the highest point, Gyatchu La pass (5,220m) en route to Lhasa via Latse. Over night stay in hotel in twin sharing room.
DAY-04 : XIGATSE – GYANTSE (3,950M),(90KM, APPROX 2 HRS DRIVE:)
Morning sight seeing to the Panchen Lama’s Tashilumpu Monastery. Drive through the Tibet’s biggest Agricultural field to Gyantse and visit to the Kumbum Stupa & Palkor Monastery. Over night stay in Hotel in twin sharing room.
DAY-05 : GYANTSE – LHASA (3650M)( 261 KM APPROX 6 HRS DRIVE:)
Full day picturesque drive along the turquoise lake Yamadrok, crossing over Kampala Pass (4794M) and the Brahmaputra river (Yarlung Tsangpo). Over night stay in hotel in twin sharing on bb basis.
DAY-06 – 07 : IN LHASA:
Two full days sight seeing tour to Lhasa including Jokhang temple, Barkhor Bazaar, Potala Palace, and Deprung and Sera Monasteries. Over night stay in Hotel in twin sharing room on bb basis .
DAY-08 : LHASA – KATHMANDU Departure
Early morning transfer to Gongaar airport for departure flight to Kathamdu.
