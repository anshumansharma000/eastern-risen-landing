# Tour To Nepal

Tour To Nepal
KATHMANDU-NAGARKOT TOUR
BACK
Tour Duration: 04 Nights/ 05 Days
Destination:Kathmandu-Nagarkot-Bhaktapur
DAY- 1 : Arrive Kathmandu
Arrive Kathmandu, you will be welcomed by our Representative who will assist you with the transfer and check-in to hotel. Afternoon sightseeing of Kathmandu durbar square and swoyambhunath. Overnight at Kathmandu.
DAY- 2 : Kathmandu-Nagarkot
After breakfast sightseeing of Kathmandu including Pashupatinath, Baudhanath, Swayambhunath, Budhanikantha (sleeping Bishnu). Afternoon drive to Nagarkot to view the sunset. Overnight at Nagarkot.
DAY- 3 : Nagarkot-Bhaktapur-Kathmandu
View the early morning sunrise and proceed for sightseeing of Bhaktapur Durbar Square and Patan Durbar Square and back to Kathmandu. Overnight at Kathmandu.
DAY- 4 : Kathmandu
After breakfast sightseeing of Dakshinkali, Kritipur and Chovar (Sacrifice on Tuesday and Saturday only). Afternoon free for own activities. Evening dinner with Nepalese Cuture show. Overnight at Kathmandu.
DAY- 5 : Kathmandu Departure
In-time transfer to Airport for onwards journey. Tour Ends.
