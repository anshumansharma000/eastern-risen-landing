# Tour To Srilanka

Tour To Srilanka
SRILANKA TOUR-V
BACK
Tour Duration: 02 Nights/03 Days
Destination:
DAY-01 : Arrival COLOMBO-KANDY
Arrival at the Bandaranaike International Airport and be met by your professional guide who will warmly welcome you and thereafter take you on your tour as per the itinerary.
Proceed direct to Kandy. You will check-in to the hotel
In the evening after having a good rest after your travel you can witness the Kandy city.
Kandy perhaps is Sri Lanka’s most beautiful town. Set 488 meters above sea level, Kandy is Sri Lanka’s most visited place after Colombo. The focal point of the town is the golden-roofed ‘Dalada Maligawa’, where the sacred tooth relic of the Buddha is enshrined. There are numerous shrines and temples in and around Kandy, where you will see rare paintings, frescoes, and stone carvings.
Late evening visit the Temple of the Tooth Relic which is located in the royal palace complex which houses the Relic of the tooth of Buddha. Since ancient times, the relic has played an important role in local politics because it is believed that whoever holds the relic holds the governance of the country. Kandy was the last capital of the Sinhalese kings and is a UNESCO world heritage site partly due to the temple. PM – Witness a cultural dance show.
These classical dance forms are associated with the performance of various rituals and ceremonies which are centuries old and are based on the folk religion and folk beliefs going back to times before the advent and acceptance of Buddhism by the Sinhalese people in the third century B.C.
Overnight stay at Kandy
DAY-02 : KANDY-NUWARA WLIYA
After breakfast at the Hotel, proceeds To Nuwara Eliya, visiting a tea factory and plantation en-route where you would see where the world’s famous “Ceylon Tea” is manufactured. Nuwara Eliya is set in the heart of tea country a beautiful town where the British succeeded in creating a replica of the English countryside, with homes in styles from Georgian to Queen Anne. Well kept lawns with hedges, an Anglican church, a famous golf course and beautiful parks give the place an air of nostalgia. Situated 6,200 feet above sea level, the air is cool and fresh – a serene retreat from the hustle and bustle of the city life. Stop at Ella. The view from Ella gap is one of the most spectacular scenic sights in Sri Lanka. The landscape falls away in a dizzy drop of 3000 feet to the eastern plains .
Overnight stay at Nuwara Eliya
DAY-03 : NUWARA ELIYA-YALA
After Breakfast proceed to Yala / Tissamaharama. Check-in to the hotel. PM - Do a safari of the Yala National Park by jeep. Yala is the largest National Park in Sri Lanka which is the home for wild elephants, wild boars, wild buffaloes, leopards, bears, deer and crocodiles. Large flocks of migratory and indigenous birds are also found here. On completion transfer back to the hotel .
Overnight stay at Yala / Tissamaharama
DAY-04 : YALA
After Breakfast proceed to Beach, en route visiting the Galle city for a city tour. Galle was once the chief port of Ceylon. It is still well known for hand-made lace. You can also visit the Galle Fort (spanning 90 acres) which was originally built by the Portuguese and later extended by the Dutch in 1663. This is a World Heritage Site.
Check-in to the hotel.
Overnight stay in a Beach Hotel
DAY-05 : YALA
After breakfast full day at leisure in the beach.
Overnight stay in a Beach Hotel
DAY-06 : YALA-COLOMBO
After Breakfast drive to Colombo, Check-in to the hotel and do a Colombo City Tour in the evening.Colombo is known as the commercial capital of Sri Lanka .
Overnight stay at Colombo
DAY-07 : COLOMBO Departure
After Breakfast, In time transfer to airport for connect flight to further destination
