# Western Odisha Glipms

Western Odisha Glipms
WESTERN ODISHA GLIPMS
BACK
Duration: 4 Nights/ 5 Days
Sambalpur-Nrusinghanath-Harishankar-Debrigarh WLS
DAY-01 : Arrival Sambalpur
Arrival at Sambalpur and check-in to the hotel. If time permits sightseeing of deer park & Samaleswari temple. Evening free for independent activities. Overnight at Sambalpur.
DAY-02 : Sambalpur-Nrusinghanath-Harishankar-Bolangir
After early breakfast drive to Nrusinghanath- hill temple. Spent few times and have a bath in the beautiful series of water falls. Proceed to an another temple Harishankar which is at the foot of the Gandhamardan hills. It dazzles with a series of waterfall on the slopes. Later on see the deer park at Harishankar and proceed to Bolangir. Overnight at Bolangir.
DAY-03 : Bolangir-Hirakud-Sambalpur
After early breakfast drive to Hirakud-the longest dam of world and larges in Asia (746 sq kms in area) and a sore line over 640 kms stands across the Mahanadi river. A 21 kms drive on the dyke offers a unique experience. Proceed to Sambalpur. Overnight at Sambalpur.
DAY-04 : Sambalpur
After breakfast full day excursion trip to Chiplima-the second hydroelectric project of the Hirakud dam. A natural fall of 80 ft high in the river Mahanadi is made use for generating electricity. Ghateswari temple-the place is mostly inhabited by fishermen whose deity is ‘Ghantei’. Huma Leaning temple-a long leaning temple being the only of its kind in Odisha, also a cenic spot on the bank of the river Mahanadi. You can cruise in the river by country boat. Overnight at Sambalpur.
DAY-05 : Sambalpur Departure.
In time transfer to Railway station for onwards journey
