# HORNBILL FESTIVAL

HORNBILL FESTIVAL
ITINERARY –II
BACK
Duration: 10 Night/11 Days
Destination: Guwahati-Jorhat-Mon-Kokokchung-Touphema-Kohima-Kaziranga N.P
DAY 01: GUWAHATI – JORHAT
Arrival at Guwahati airport,drive to Jorhat (07 hrs), after a brief welcome you will be escorted to hotel, evening at leisure. Overnight at Leisure.
DAY 02: JORHAT – MON
Today after breakfast drive to Mon - Mon – located at an altitude of 897.64 meters above sea level is the land of Konyak Nagas, they were one of the great warriors among Nagas. Afternoon go for a walk through the Mon Market. Overnight at Mon.
DAY 03: MON
After breakfast, drive to Shangnyu village - this village is ruled by the Chief Angh is a prominent village in Mon district. There is a wonderful wooden monument measuring 8 feet in height and 12 feet in breadth- believed to be constructed by heavenly angels, on way visit Mon village. After lunch drive to Longwa village (41 kms) which is in the border of India and Burma. In this village one can also see the traditional from of making opium. Evening at leisure. Overnight at Mon.
DAY 04: MON – MOKOKCHUNG
After breakfast we drive to Mokokchung (220 kms / 9 hrs approx) located at an altitude of 1325 meters above sea level, this picturesque town is the cultural center of the Ao tribes. The Ao Nagas is famous for their colourful dances. Overnight at Mokokchung.
DAY 05: MOKOKCHUNG – TOUPHEMA
After breakfast visit one village and then drive to Tuophema village (167 kms / 05 hrs) . Enroute visit the Rengma and Lotha tribal villages. Over night at Tuophema village.
DAY 06: TOUPHEMA – KOHIMA
After breakfast we proceed to Kohima – the capital of Nagaland (40 kms / 02 hrs), to attain the opening ceremony of Hornbill festival.
HORN BILL FESTIVAL
The Horn Bill Festival of Nagaland represents the Bicultural extravaganza of the northeast Indian state. The Nagaland festival provides tourists the opportunity to glimpse of the festive celebrations of the state. The festival starts in the first week of December every year and it continues for a week. Nagaland is the land of festivals with one festival following the other all through the year.
Evening at leisure. Overnight at Kohima.
DAY 07: KOHIMA
Full day attain Hornbill Festival. Overnight at Kohima.
DAY 08: KOHIMA
Full day attain Hornbill festival. Overnight at Kohima.
DAY 09: KOHIMA–KAZIRANGA NATIONAL PARK
After breakfast drive to Kaziranga National Park (217 KMS / 07 HRS).
Kaziranga National Park
In the heart of Assam, covering an area of 430 sq-kms, is inhabited by the world's largest population of one-horned rhinoceroses, as well as many mammals, including tigers (sighting of tigers are difficult), elephants, hog & swamp deer, water buffaloes and almost 478 species of birds (both migratory and residents). In 1985, it was declared a World Heritage Site by UNESCO for its unique natural environment.
Evening free at leisure. Overnight at Kaziranga.
DAY 10: KAZIRANGA NATIONAL PARK
After an early wake up, you will be taken to the riding point in the Central Range (Kohoro) for an Elephant Ride. After the ride return back to the lodge, having breakfast at 7 am drive to the Central Rang (Kohora) of the park for a jeep safari. After lunch at 2.15 there will be another Jeep safari in the Western Range (Baguri) of the park. Evening at leisure. Overnight at Kaziranga.
DAY 11: KAZIRANGA NATIONAL PARK – GUWAHATI
After breakfast drive to Guwahati airport for onward destination.
