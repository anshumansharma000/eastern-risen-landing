# Spiritual & Shaman Tour

Spiritual & Shaman Tour
SHAMAN TOUR
BACK
Duration: 09 Nights/ 10 Days
Places covered: Delhi-Guwahati-Kolkata-Bhubaneswar-Konark-Puri
DAY-1 : Arrival DELHI
Arrival at Delhi. Transfer to hotel. After fresh up, half day sight seeing of Old Delhi. O/N-Delhi.
DAY-2 : DELHI
After breakfast in time transfer to Airport for connecting flight to Guwahati. Arrival at Guwahati, transfer to hotel. Afternoon boating inside the river Brahmaputra. Visiting to the Umanand temple (Lord Shiva). O/N-Guwahati.
DAY-3 : GUWAHATI
After breakfast visit to Kamakhya temple, experience the Kumari pujan (girls’ worship). Visit to Kamakhya group of temples.After lunch visit to Sualkhosi-Silk textile village. O/N-Guwahati.
DAY-4 : GUWAHATI-KOLKATA
After breakfast visit to Vasistha Ashram, Hajo,Temple of Madan kamdev, Creamation ground of Bhutnath. In time transfer to
Airport for connecting flight to Kolkata. Arrival at Kolkata and transfer to hotel. O/N-Kolkata.
DAY-5 : KOLKATA
After breakfast full day sightseeing of Kolkata including Kalighat, Dakhineswar Kali, Belur Math & Ramakrishna Mission. O/N-Kolkata.
DAY-6 : KOLKATA- BHUBANSWAR
In time transfer to Airport for connecting flight to Bhubaneswar. Arrival at Bhubaneswar and check in to the hotel. After
refreshment sightseeing of temples like Lingaraj, Mukteswar, Rajarani & 64 Yogini temple. Evening meditation in a Yoga centre. O/N-Bhubaneswar.
DAY-7 : BHUBANESWAR
Early morning practice of meditation & yoga in a yoga centre. After breakfast visit to a village to experience tantric puja. Afternoon free for independent activities. Evening meditation in a yoga centre. O/N-Bhubaneswar.
DAY-8 : BHUBANESWAR-KONARK-PURI
After breakfast proceed to Puri. En route visit to the Dhauli-the Buddhist peace pagoda, pipli appliqué market, Sun temple Konark (UNESCO World heritage site), Evening free for independent activities.
DAY-9 : PURI
After breakfast visit to the temple of universal God Lord Jagannath. Then visit to the cremation ground, Loknath temple. Evening meditation in a yoga centre. O/N-Puri.
DAY-10 : PURI Departure
In time transfer to Airport to connect flight for onwards journey.
