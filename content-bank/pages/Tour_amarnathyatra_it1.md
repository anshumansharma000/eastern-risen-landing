# Amarnath Yatra

Amarnath Yatra
Amarnath Darshan Via Pahalgam
BACK
DAY- 1 : Srinagar airport – Pahalgam 100 Kms
On arrival meeting at the airport and three hours drive will take you to Pahalgam 2440 Mtrs (vale of Kashmir) on the way visit Saffron fields, Avantipur ruins and Anantnag sulpher spring. Pahalgam is the most famous place for Indian Film Industry. Enjoy the nature charm of the valley. Overnight at Houseboat.
Day 02 : Pahalgam - Chandanwari - Sheshnag 13 Kms drive and 14 Kms trek
Leave early morning for and 13 Kms drive to Chandanwari immediately steep ascending Pisoo Ghati- 13500 ft which is the base. Overnight at Camping.
Day 03 : Sheshnag 3377 Mtrs - Panchtarni 14 Kms - Amarnath - Baltal - Sonmarg
Start early morning for a steep ascent upto Mahagunas ( The great Viper 14500 fts) with ups and downs walk upto the Panchtarni where you will stop for lunch and immediately start another 6 Kms trek to Amarnath ji Holy cave (3953 M) where you can see Ice Lingam. This is the Holy cave for Hidus and thousands of people visit the place during the full moon day. From Amarnath steep descent towards to Baltal 4 hours walk and short drive to Sonmarg. Overnight at Hotel.
DAY- 4 : Baltal- Sonmarg – Srinagar(113 Kms drive)
After breakfast leave for Srinagar airport which will take you about 3 hrs drive to reach Srinagar airport. Tour ends here..
