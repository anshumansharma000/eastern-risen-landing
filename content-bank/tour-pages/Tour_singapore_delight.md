# Singapore Delight

Singapore Delight
SINGAPORE DELIGHT
BACK
Duration: 3 Nights/4 Days
Singapore-Jargon bird Park
DAY-01 :Arrival in Singapore
Arrival in Singapore. You will be welcome by our Representative who will assist you with the transfer and check-in to hotel. In the evening, proceed for an exciting Night safari adventure. This 40-hectare park comes alive after dark, with over 1200 animals engaging in their fascinating and nightly rituals. Overnight at hotel.
DAY-02 : Singapore City Tour and Sentosa island with underwater world
After breakfast at hotel, proceed for a panoramic half-day city tour. Driving pass the colonial buildings, government houses, Little India and Chinatown, Singapore’s famous icon, the marine. See the world’s largest fountain with a base area of 1683 square meters-the “Fountain of Wealth”. in the afternoon, Visit sentosa Island including a trip to underwater world, home to more than 2500 fishes. Overnight at hotel.(Meals: Breakfast)
DAY-03 :Jargon bird Park and Shopping
After breakfast at hotel, proceed for Jurong Bird park ,home to over 9000 birds. Take a ride on air conditioned panorail for a panoramic view of the park and into a tropical world of jungle mist at the waterfall Aviary. Afterward, enjoy a spectacular show where you will meet all the stars of the park- snowy the Swan, crazy Bird, Toukie the Toucan and many more. The evening is free for shopping and other leisure activities. Overnight at hotel (Meals: Breakfast)
DAY-04 :Depart from Singapore
After breakfast at hotel, enjoy the day at leisure for last minute shopping and other activities of choice. Check out from hotel and transfer to the airport.
