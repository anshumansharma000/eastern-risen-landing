# South India Tour

South India Tour
SOUTHERN HERITAGE
BACK
Duration: 11 Night/ 12 Days
Destination: Bengaluru-Hassan-Mysore-Trichy-Tanjore-Madurai-Kancheepuram-Mahabalipuram-Chennai
DAY-01 : Arrival- BENGALURU
Arrival at Bengaluru. You will be greeted by our representative who will assist you to board your vehicle to Hotel. Check-in to the Hotel. Halfday city tour includes,Lal Bagh, Gole Temple, the Government Building, Vishveshwara Industrial and Technology Museum and the Ulsoor Lake. Overnight stay at Bengaluru.
DAY-02 : BENGALURU- HASSAN ( 194 kms)
After breakfast, drive to Hassan(194 kms/3.5 hrs) .After noon sightseeing of Chennakeswara Temple at Belur, and the Hoyasaleswara Temple at Halebid. Overnight stay at Hassan.
DAY-03 : HASSAN-MYSORE (118 kms)
After breakfast, drive to Mysore (118 kms/2 ½ hrs). Enroute visit to Srabanbelagola, Srirangam. Arrival at Mysore. Check into Hotel. Evening free for independent activities. Overnight at Mysore.
DAY-04 : MYSORE
After breakfast, full day sightseeing of Mysore including the magnificent Maharaja’s Palace, Chamundi Temple, and the Art Gallery, visit the fruits and flower markets. Overnight stay at Mysore.
DAY-05 : MYSORE-TRICHY ( 276 kms)
After breakfast, drive to Trichy (276 kms/5-6 hrs). Check in to the Hotel. Afternoon free for individual activities. Overnight stay at Trichy.
DAY-06 : TRICHY & TANJORE
After breakfast, full day sight seeing of Trichy & Tanjore including, Rock Fort, Srirangam, one of the largest temple of God Vishnu, and the Jambkeswaram temple of Lord Shiva submerged in water. Subramaya temple of Tanjore. Overnight stay at Trichy.
DAY-07 : TRICHY – MADURAI (140 kms)
After breakfast, drive to Madurai (140 kms/2.5-3 hrs). Enroute visit to Cocoanut Rope work. Afternoon free for individual activities. Overnight stat at Madurai.
DAY-08 : MADURAI
After breakfast, fullday sightseeing of Madurai including Meenakshi Temple,Nayak Palace and the daily village market. Evening witness the night garland ceremony at meenakshi temple. Overnight stay at Madurai.
DAY-09 : MADURAI-CHENNAI (453 kms)
After breakfast ,in time transfer to airport for connect flight to Chennai. Arrival at Chennai & transfer to Hotel. Evening free for independent activities. Overnight stay at Chennai.
DAY-10 : CHENNAI
After breakfast, full day excursion to Kanchipuram and Mahabalipuram.
Kancheepuram : known as the Golden city of a thousand temples. This is one of India’s sacred cities. Many early styles of temple architectural design; the Five Radhas-a group of monolithic monuments and animal figures carved out of solid rock; the Shore Temple, lashed by the waves of the Sea; and the huge frescoe; ‘The Penance of Bhagirathi’.
Mahabalipuram : An ancient coastal port South of Madras, here you will see many sculptures and temples of typical South India architectural designs; the five Radhas - A group of Monolithic monuments of animal figures carved out of solid rock; the shore temple, lashed by the waves of the sea, and the Huge frescoes; the penance of Bhagirath. Overnight Chennai.
DAY-11 : CHENNAI
After breakfast, fullday city tour including cathedral and the Fort of St. George. Visit the Marina Beach- second longest beach in the world, its an elegant promenade; Fort St. George- the first foothold of British from where Robert Clive started the expansion of British Rule. Evening free for Indipendent activities. Overnight at Hotel.
DAY-12 : CHENNAI- Departure
After breakfast, in-time transfer to airport for connect flight to further destination.
