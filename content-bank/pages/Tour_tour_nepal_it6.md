# Tour To Nepal

Tour To Nepal
PASHUPATINATH & MUKTINATH TOUR-I
BACK
Tour Duration: 06 Nights/ 07 Days
Destination:Kathmandu-Pokhara-Jomsom
DAY- 1 : Arrive Kathmandu
Arrive Kathmandu, you will be welcomed by our Representative who will assist you with the transfer and check-in to hotel, rest of the day leisure for shopping. Overnight at Kathmandu.
DAY- 2 : Kathmandu
After breakfast full day sightseeing of Kathmandu including Pashupatinath, Baudhanath, Swayambhunath, Budhanikantha (sleeping Bishnu) Durbar Square (optional tour Casino). Overnight at Kathmandu.
DAY- 3 : Kathmandu-Pokhara (210 kms/4.5 hrs)
After breakfast drive to Pokhara (210 kms/4.5 hrs), en-route visit Manokamana temple (only possible by Cable Car). For Bus, directly to Pokhara. Evening free for independent activities. Overnight at Pokhara.
DAY- 4 : Pokhara
After breakfast, full day sightseeing tour of Pokhara (Fewa lake, Devis falls, Seti river National Museum, Mahendra Cave, Gupteswhwor Mahadev Cave and many more on the way etc. Overnight at Pokhara.
DAY- 5 : Pokhara-(fly)Jomsom
After breakfast drive to airport to catch the flight to Jomsom and jeep drive to Muktinath Darshan and have holy bath, worship to Lord Narayan, visit Jwalamukhi and drive back to Jomsom. Overnight at Jomsom.
DAY- 6 : Jomsom-Pokhara-Kathmandu
After breakfast, fly back to Pokhara and transfer to Kathmandu. Evening at leisure. Overnight at Kathmandu.
DAY- 7 : Kathmandu Departure
In time transfer to Airport for onwards journey. Tour Ends.
