# Odisha Golden Triangle

Odisha Golden Triangle
ODISHA GOLDEN TRIANGLE
BACK
Duration: 3 Nights/ 4 Days
Bhubaneswar-Konark-Chilika (Satapada)-Puri
DAY-01 : Arrival Bhubaneswar
Arrival at Bhubaneswar. Check-in to the hotel. After noon orientation tour of Bhubaneswar including temples of Lingaraj, Mukteswar, Rajarani etc. Over night at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-02 : Bhubaneswar-Konark-Puri
After breakfast drive to Puri. En-route visit through Dhauli-the Buddhist circuit, Pipili-the appliqué village, Sun temple at Konark-the world UNESCO heritage monument, Chandrabhaga beach, Ramchandi-a picnic spot & temple. Arrival at Puri. Spent the evening at golden beach Puri. Overnight at Puri.
DAY-03 : Puri-Chilika-Bhubaneswar
Early in morning drive to Satapada (One end of Chilika Lagoon). Cruise in the largest brakish water lagoon in Asia and explore Sea mouth, Rajhans Island, rare Irrawady dolphins, local birds etc. Back to Puri. Late after noon proceed to Bhubaneswar, en-route visit through Raghurajpur artisan village. Overnight at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-04 : Bhubaneswar Departure
First half sightseeing of Bhubaneswar including Khandagiri & Udayagiri caves, Tribal museum. If time permits visit to Nandankanan zoological park (popular for white tiger). Transfer to railway station/airport for onwards journey.
