# Odisha Golden Triangle-I

Odisha Golden Triangle-I
ODISHA GOLDEN TRIANGLE-I
BACK
Duration: 4 Nights/ 5 Days
Bhubaneswar-Ratnagiri-Lalitgiri-Udaygiri-Pipli-Konark-Puri
DAY-01 : Arrival Bhubaneswar
Arrival at Bhubaneswar. Transfer to the hotel. Half day city tour of Bhubaneswar including Bhaskareswar Temple, Rajarani Temple, Brahmeswar Temple,Vaitaldeol & Lingaraja Temple. (From View Point as Non Hindus are not allowed inside the Temple), Mukteswar & Parsurameswar Temple,Bindu Sagar. Overnight Bhubaneswar
DAY-02 : BHUBANESWAR-LALITGIRI-RATNAGIRI-UDAYGIRI-BHUBANESWAR
After breakfast, drive to Ratnagiri & Udayagiri group of Budhist monuments. Experience the different types of stupas & visit to ASI Museum.Visit to Lalitgiri group of monuments and Langudi.( If Time Permits).Visit to Gopal Pur handloom village.(If Time Permit) Over night Bhubaneswar.
DAY-03 : BHUBANESWAR-PIPLI-KONARK-PURI
After breakfast drive to Puri, En route visit to Dhauli-the Buddhist Peace Pagoda,64-Yogini Temple(Unique in Temple Architecture) Pipli-the appliqué work.,Sun temple at Konark (The UNESCO world heritage monument), Chandrabhaga beach. Arrival at Puri.Evening free for independent activity. Over night Puri.
DAY-04 : PURI-CHILIKA- RAGHURAJ PUR-BHUBANESWAR
Early morning sunrise at Puri sea beach. After early breakfast drive to Satapada (one end of Chilika lagoon) cruise in the largest brakish water lagoon of Asia and explore rare Irrawady dolphins, sea mouth, local & migratory birds.After lunch drive back to Bhubaneswar, en route visit through Raghuraj Pur.(UNESCO HERITAGE VILLAGE). Overnight Bhubaneswar
DAY-05 : BHUBANESWAR - Departure
After breakfast sightseeing of Tribal & State museum (Monday closed), Khandagiri & Udayagiri Caves.(jain Monuments) In time transfer to Airport for onwards journey.
