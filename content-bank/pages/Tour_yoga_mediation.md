# Yoga and Meditation Tour

Yoga and Meditation Tour
Yoga and Meditation Tour (SPIRITUAL TOUR )
BACK
DAY- 01
Arrival at Bhubaneswar, Traditional welcome, transfer to the hotel. Half day sightseeing of Bhubaneswar including Lingraj Temple (Non-Hindus are not allowed inside the temple), Rajarani Temple, Mukteshwar Temple and Parsurameswar Temple .Overnight Bhubaneswar.
DAY- 02
Early morning transfer to Yoga Ashram, enchanting of Mantras, practice of Asanas, Pranayam and Meditation. After breakfast, city tour and afternoon free for independent activity. Overnight Bhubaneswar
DAY- 03
Early morning transfer to Yoga Ashram, enchanting of Mantras, practice of Asanas, Pranayam and Meditation. After breakfast, visit to different Ashrams, afternoon visit to Khandagiri and Udaygiri group of monuments. Evening visit temples to see the evening prayer. Overnight Bhubaneswar.
DAY -04
Early morning transfer to Yoga Ashram, enchanting of Mantras, practice of Asanas, Pranayam and Meditation. After breakfast visit to Lalitgiri, Udaigiri, Ratnagiri group of Buddhist monuments. Drive back to Bhubaneswar, overnight at Bhubaneswar.
DAY -05
Early morning transfer to Yoga Ashram, enchanting of Mantras, practice of Asanas, Pranayam and Meditation. After breakfast drive to Puri.En route visit to Dhauli peace pagoda, 64-Yogini Temple, Pipili appliqué work. Afternoon visit to Jagannath Temple (Non-Hindus are not allowed inside the Temple) Evening free for independent activities.Overnight Puri.
DAY -06
Early morning transfer to Yoga Ashram, enchanting of Mantras, practice of Asanas, Pranayam and Meditation. After breakfast visit to Raghurajpur the heritage village. Raghurajpur is known for its special type of paintings called the Pattachitra. This is a traditional Indian painting, which is done on cloth. Afternoon visit to different Ashrams, evening Arati on the beach (Evening Spiritual Ceremony). Overnight Puri.
DAY -07
Early morning transfer to Yoga Ashram, enchanting of Mantras, practice of Asanas, Pranayam and Meditation. After breakfast excursion to Konark and Chilika Lake (it is a shallow, brackish-water lake on the eastern coast of Odisha), evening free for independent activities. Overnight Puri.
DAY -08
Early morning transfer to Yoga Ashram, enchanting of Mantras, practice of Asanas, Pranayam and Meditation. After breakfast, In time transfer to railway station/ airport for onwards journey.
