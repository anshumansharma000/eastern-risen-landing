# North India Tour

North India Tour
SPLENDID KASHMIR
BACK
Duration: 5 Night/6 Days
Destination: Srinagar-Pahalgaon-Gulmarg-Sonmarg
DAY-1 : Arrival SRINAGAR
Arrival at Srinagar Airport. You will be greeted by our representative who will assist you to board the vehicle. Check-in to house boat. Afternoon Sikara ride in Dal Lake. O/N stay at Srinagar (House boat).
DAY-2 : SRINAGAR-PAHALGAON(96 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Pahalgaon (2130 mts/96 kms/3.5 hrs). Arrival at Pahalgaon which is the Kashmir’s most popular destination, surrounded by high mountains, dense forests, high mountain lakes, valley, Kolhai glacier. Sightseeing of Shesh Nag, Chandanwari (route to the skiing, pony trails, angling, horse riding, trekking etc. O/N at Pahalgaon.
DAY-3 : PAHALGAON-GULMARG (148 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Gulmarg (148 kms/5 hrs) known as the ‘Meadow of Flowers’. Gulmarg- historically a favourite haunt of Emperor Jahangir.Gulmarg today is not merely a mountain resort of exceptional beauty- it also turns into india’s premier ski resort in winter. After Lunch enjoy gondola rides, pony rides etc. and enjoy the scenic beauty. O/N stay at Gulmarg.
DAY-4 : GULMARG-SRINAGAR (52 kms)
For noon proceed to Srinagar (52 kms/2 hrs). After Lunch sightseeing of Mughal garden. Evening free for independent activities. O/N stay at rinagar.
DAY-5 : SRINGAR-SONMARG (82 kms)
Early morning proceed to Sonmarg the ‘Meadow of Gold’ (82 kms/4 hrs). The drive through this valley presents a spectacular facet of the Kashmir countryside. Sonmarg is surrounded by colourful mountains and thick pine forests. Enjoy a pony ride on the glacier. Come back to Srinagar. O/N stay at Srinagar.
DAY-6 : SRINAGAR Departure
In-time transfer to Airport for onwards journey.
