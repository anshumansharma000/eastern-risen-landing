# Tour To Nepal

Tour To Nepal
KATHMANDU CULTURE TOUR-I
BACK
Tour Duration: 04 Nights/ 05 Days
Destination: Kathmandu-Patan
DAY- 1 : Arrive Kathmadu
Arrive Kathmandu, you will be welcomed by our Representative who will assist you with the transfer and check-in to hotel, rest of the day leisure for shopping. Overnight at Kathmandu.
DAY- 2 : Kathmandu
After breakfast full day sightseeing of Kathmandu including Pashupatinath, Baudhanath, Swayambhunath, Budhanikantha (sleeping Bishnu) Durbar Square (optional tour Casino). Overnight at Kathmandu.
DAY- 3 : Kathmandu-Patan-Bhaktapur
After breakfast drive to Patan & Bhaktapur city. Sightseeing of Patan & Bhaktapur city. See Sunset and Sunrise at Nagarkot. Overnight at Bhaktapur.
DAY- 4 : Bhaktapur - Kathmandu
After breakfast drive back to Kathmandu and go for shopping and free for independent activities. Overnight at Kathmandu.
DAY- 5 : Kathmandu Departure
In-time transfer to Airport for onwards journey. Tour Ends
