# Budhist Tour

Budhist Tour
NORTHERN BUDDHIST TOUR
BACK
Duration: 8 Nights/9 Days
Destination: Delhi-Gaya-Bodhgaya-Rajgiri-Nalanda-Varanasi-Gorakhpur-Kushinagar-Lumbini-Agra
DAY - 1 : Arrival at DELHI
Arrival at Delhi. Traditional welcome. Transfer to Railway Station for Gaya. Over night in Train.
DAY-2 : GAYA-BODH GAYA
Arrival at Gaya Railway station. Transfer to Bodh gaya. Check in to hotel. Full day sightseeing of Bodh gaya including Mahabodhi temple & Niranjana river, Thai temple, Japanese temple and Buddha statue. Overnight at Bodh gaya.
DAY-3 : BODHGAYA- RAJGIRI-NALANDA - GAYA
After early breakfast, drive to Rajgiri. Arrival at Rajgir. Sightseeing of Bimbimsara Jail, Gridhakut hill, Venuvan. After lunch, proceed to Nalanda world famous University & Nalanda Museum. Drive back to Gaya Railway station. Board on train for Varanasi. Overnight in Train.
DAY-4 : VARANASI
Arrival at Varanasi. Transfer to hotel. After break fast, full day sightseeing of Sarnath, Dhamekh stupa, Sarnath museum, The Ashokan pillar, Mulgandha kuti vihar. Evening witness Ganga Arati .Transfer to Railway station. Board on Train for Gorakhapur. Overnight in Train.
DAY-5 : GORAKH PUR – KUSHI NAGAR
Arrival at Gorakhpur. Transfer to Hotel. After breakfast visit to Mahaparinirvan temple, Rambhar stupa, Mata kutir temple. Evening witness chanting and meditation session. Overnight stay at Kushinagar.
DAY-6 : KUSHINAGAR – LUMBINI- GORAKHA PUR
After early breakfast drive to Lumbini (Nepal). Arrival at Lumbini and sightseeing of Maya Devi temple, Ashokan pillar and Pushkarni lake. Drive back to Gorakhapur. Board on Train to Gonda. Overnight in Train.
DAY-7 : GONDA- SRABASTI- GONDA-AGRA
Arrival at Gonda Railway station. Transfer to Sravasti. After breakfast sightseeing of Jetvana Vihara, Pakki kuti & Sehat mahet, Thai temple. Drive back to Gonda Railway station, Board on Train to Agra. Overnight in Train.
DAY-8 : AGRA
Arrival at Agra. Visit to Taj Mahal, Agra Fort. Afternoon free for Independent activity.
Overnight stay at Agra.
DAY-9 : AGRA – DELHI Departure
After breakfast drive to Delhi & In time transfer to airport for further destination.
