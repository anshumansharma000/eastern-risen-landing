# North India Tour

North India Tour
GOLDEN TRIANGLE
BACK
Duration: 6 Night/7 Days
Destination: Delhi-Jaipur-Agra
DAY-1 : Arrival DELHI
Arrival at Delhi Airport/Railway station. You will be greeted by our representative who will assist you to board the vehicle. Check-in to the Hotel. Spend the evening at leisure.
DAY-2 : DELHI
After breakfast, full day sightseeing of Old & New Delhi including Humayun’s Tomb, QutubMinar, Rajpath, IndiaGate and Parliament House, Redfort, Rajpath, Jama Masjid etc. O/N stay at Delhi.
DAY-3 : DELHI-JAIPUR (262 kms)
After breakfast drive to Jaipur (262 kms/ 5 hrs). Afternoon sightseeing of City palace, observatory (JantarMantar), Hawa Mahal (Palace of Wind). O/N stay at Jaipur.
DAY-4 : JAIPUR
After breakfast, visit to Amber Fort on elephant back. Afternoon free for independent activity or explore the colourful Jaipur market. O/N stay at Jaipur
DAY-5 : JAIPUR-FATEHPURSIKRI-AGRA (240 kms)
After breakfast drive to Agra (240 kms/4-5 hrs) . En route visit Fatehpur Sikri. O/N stay at Agra.
DAY-6 : AGRA-DELHI ( 210 kms)
After breakfast full day sightseeing of Taj Mahal, Agra Fort. Afternoon drive back to Delhi ( 210 kms/4-5 hrs). Visit through Mathura Vridaban. O/N stay at Delhi
DAY-7 : AGRA Departure
In-time transfer to Airport/Railway station for onwards journey.
