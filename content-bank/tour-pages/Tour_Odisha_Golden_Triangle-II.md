# Odisha Golden Triangle-II

Odisha Golden Triangle-II
ODISHA GOLDEN TRIANGLE-II
BACK
Duration: 4 Nights/ 5 Days
Bhubaneswar-Chilika (Satapada)-Puri
DAY-01 : Arrival Bhubaneswar
Arrival at Bhubaneswar. Check-in to the hotel. After noon orientation tour of Bhubaneswar. Evening free for independent activities. Over night at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-02 : Bhubaneswar-Konark-Puri
After breakfast drive to Puri. En-route visit through Dhauli-the Buddhist circuit, Pipili-the appliqué village, Sun temple at Konark-the world UNESCO heritage monuments, Chandrabhaga beach, Ramchandi-a picnic spot & temple. Arrival at Puri. Spent the evening at golden beach Puri. Overnight at Puri.
DAY-03 : Puri-Chilika-Bhubaneswar
Early in morning drive to Satapada (One end of Chilika Lagoon). Cruise in the largest brakish water lagoon in Asia and explore Sea mouth, Rajhans Island, rare Irrawady dolphins, local birds etc. Back to Puri. Late after noon visit to the Jagannath temple (non hindus are not allowed) and spent there till evening. Overnigh at Puri.
DAY-04 : Bhubaneswar
After breakfast full day sightseeing of Bhubaneswar including temples like Lingaraj, Rajarani, Mukteswar, 64 Yogini etc., Khandagiri & Udayagiri caves, Tribal museum, Nandankanan Zoological Park-popular for white tiger. Overnight at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-05 :Bhubaneswar Departure
In time transfer to Airport/Railway station for onwards journey.
