# East India Tour

East India Tour
EASTERN WILD ADVENTURE
BACK
Duration: 9 Nights/ 10 Days
Destination: Bhubaneswar-Bhitarkanika N.P-Similipal N.P-Chandipur
DAY-1 : Arrival BHUBANESWAR
Arrival at Bhubaneswar Airport/Railway station. You will be greeted by our office representative who will assist you to board your vehicle. Transfer to Hotel. Afternoon Orientation tour of Bhubaneswar. Overnight stay at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-2 : BHUBANESWAR-GUPTI (170 kms)
After breakfast, drive to Ratnagiri & Udayagiri group of monuments(120 kms/3 hrs). See the art & architect of the ruined Buddhist monuments, Museum-a large collection of excavation (closed on Friday). Then visit to Lalitgiri group of monuments & the stone work. After lunch at way side Dhaba proceed to Gupti. Evening enjoy the nature trek and feel the continues cool air. Overnight stay at
Gupti.
DAY-3 : BHITARKANIKA NATIONAL PARK
After breakfast proceed to Pentha sea beach and Satabhaya ethnic village. Spent the half day in this lonely place and feel the cool nature. Have your packet lunch. Come back to Gupti. Overnight stay at Gupti.
DAY-4 : BHITARKANIKA NATIONAL PARK
After breakfast cruise in different creeks like Gadhirapal, Suajore etc to spot Crocodiles, then visti to Bagagahana ( Bird Sanctuary) where during winter you can see many migratory birds, Sikarakathi ( hunting tower of ancient king), Shiv temple. Have your lunch at Dangamal forest restaurant and see crocodile breeding centre, Museum, deer, Water lizard etc. Overnight stay at Gupti.
DAY-5 : GUPTI-LULUNG (SIMILIPAL N.P) (295 kms)
After breakfast, proceed to Lulung (Similipal N.P) (295 kms/6 hrs). Have your lunch at road side Dhaba. Arrival Similipal N.P and check in to the Forest Lodge. After noon enjoy the scenic beauty and feel the sound of the nature. Overnight stay at Forest Guest house.
DAY-6 : SIMILIPAL NATIONAL PARK
After breakfast start for Jungle ride. Sightseeing of Jaranda water fall (181 mtr) where a watch tower with glass house available for observing wild beauty of forest, Nawana- a green valley with crop lands. One additional attraction of this place is the lifestyle of the tribals residing in the valley. Barehipani water fall (217 mtr) where river Budhabalanga runs to the plain land. Chahala- where sighting of wild animals are more. Over night stay at Forest Guest house.
DAY-7 : LULUNG-CHANDIPUR (101 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Chandipur-on-sea (101 kms/2.5 hrs). En route visit through Khirachora Gopinath temple, Balaramgadi fishing harber. After noon enjoy at the unique beach (where sea recedes up to 5 kms and again come to shore two time in a day). Over night stay at Chandipur-on-sea.
DAY-8 : CHANDIPUR-BHUBANESWAR (216 kms)
After breakfast proceed to Panchalingeswar hill temple & Stream ( 46 kms/1 hrs). Then proceed to Bhubanewar (200 kms/4 hrs). Have your lunch at road side dhaba. Evening free for independent activities. Overnight stay at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-9 : BHUBANESWAR
After breakfast full day city tour including temples like Lingaraj, Rajarani, Mukteswar, Khandagiri & Udayagiri rock cut caves, Tribal museum & Nandankanan Zoological park. Overnight stay at Bhubaneswar.
DAY-10 : Departure Bhubaneswar
In time transfer to Airport/Railway station for onwards journey.
