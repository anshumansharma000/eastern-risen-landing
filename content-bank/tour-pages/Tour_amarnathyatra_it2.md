# Amarnath Yatra

Amarnath Yatra
Amarnath Darshan Via Sonmarg/Baltal
BACK
Day 01 : Srinagar airport – Sonmarg – Baltal 113 Kms
Drive to Sonmarg which is the most beautiful drive from Srinagar. On the way we stop at many beautiful spot. Continue drive to Sonmarg 2690 Mtrs (Meadow of Gold) by lunch time you will reach Sonmarg continue drive to Baltal which will took you 1 Hour from Sonmarg. Overnight in tents..
Day 02 : Baltal – Amarnath – Baltal 32 Kms return
Leave early morning for Amarnath Holy cave (3953 M) where you can see Ice Lingam. This is the Holy cave for Hidus and thousands of people visit the place during the full moon day. From Amarnath steep descent towards to Baltal 4 hours walk. Overnight in tents.
Day 03 : Baltal- Sonmarg – Srinagar(113 Kms drive)
After breakfast leave for Srinagar. Arrive Srinagar on Lunch time. Afternoon free for personal activities . Overnight at Houseboat.
Day 04 : Srinagar
Transfer in time to the airport to connect onwards destination flight.
