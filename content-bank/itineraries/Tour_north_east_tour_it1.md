# North East Tour (Sikkim Tour).

North East Tour (Sikkim Tour).
EASTERN HIMALAYAN-I
BACK
Tour Duration: 2 Nights/ 3 Days
Destination: Gangtak-Darjeeling
DAY- 01 : Arrival NJP RAILWAY STATION /BAGDOGRA AIRPORT - GANGTOK
Arrival at NJP Railway Station / (IXB) Bagdogra Airport, (500Ft / 150Mts) will be met by our office Executive who will assist you to board your vehicle to Gangtok (5500 Ft /1677 Mts, 130 Kms / 04 to 05 Hrs). Check in and over night stay at Gangtok.
DAY- 02 : GANGTOK
After breakfast, in the morning, start for excursion to Tshangu Lake - situated at a height of 12400Ft / 3780 Mts / 43 kms in 3 hrs one way, with an average depth of 50 ft. The cool water of the Lake is perfectly attuned with the scenic beauty around. Back to Gangtok and Overnight stay at Gangtok.
DAY- 03 : GANGTOK - DARJEELING
After breakfast, transfer to Darjeeling (7380 Ft / 2250 Mts, 115 Kms / 05 to 06 Hrs). Afternoon go for a half-day sight seeing of Japanese Temple, Peace pagoda, Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park & Himalayan Mountaineering Institute (Closed on Thursday), Tenzing & Gombu Rock, Tibetan Refugee Self-Help Center (Closed on Sunday) & Tea Estate. Overnight stay at Darjeeling.
DAY -04 : DARJEELING - NJP RAILWAY / IXB AIRPORT Departure
Early morning tour to Tiger Hills (8364 Ft / 2550 Mts at around 4 AM) to view sunrise over Kanchendzonga Peak (subject to clear weather). On way back visit Ghoom Monastery, Batasia Loop. After breakfast in time transfer to NJP Railway Station / (IXB) Bagdogra Airport (100 kms / 03 to 04 hrs ) for your onward connection.
